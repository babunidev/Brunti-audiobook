# Concept — *Brunti* (working title)

Through this book, I want Morkahja's encounters to become a connected fantasy adventure, seen most often through a young Grimnir warrior whose body and culture are rooted in living stone, metal, resonance and moving Skylands.

Rather than replaying a game, the book will find a narrative place for **all** its source encounters. Brunti need not witness them all: strangers' stories, oral accounts, old records, songs and dreams can reveal distant lives. Conflict matters, but so do rest, travel, healing, community, trade, craft and ordinary beauty.

## Creative direction established by Babuni

- A **young female Grimnir warrior** leads the novel.
- Her name comes from game avatar data; **Brunti** is the provisional title.
- All source encounters should eventually have a meaningful literary use.
- Adaptation is free to move beyond game solutions or outcomes.
- Work step by step, beginning with a documented foundation rather than prematurely writing the whole book.

## Working artistic principles

One main narrative needs human-scale desires, relationships, consequences and change; it must not become 203 disconnected quests. The established world remains canonical through MorkahjaWorld, while this book's plot and new personal histories remain proposals until approved.

See [Story bible](story/STORY_BIBLE.md), [Encounter atlas](story/ENCOUNTER_ATLAS.md), [Decisions](docs/DECISIONS.md) and [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

No final title, definitive plot, fixed chapter count, completed manuscript, recorded audiobook or release is claimed.
