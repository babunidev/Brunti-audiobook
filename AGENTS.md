# Brunti audiobook — project and AI collaboration rules

**Human creative:** Babuni. **Purpose:** Literary adaptation and audiobook-production workspace, separate from Morkahja world canon.

Read this file, `docs/DOCUMENTATION.md`, `docs/SOURCES.md`, and relevant story files before changing anything. Check current repository state and source revisions.

## Source authority

- [MorkahjaWorld](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld) is the established lore archive for mythology, geography, cultures, divine laws and open mysteries.
- [Morkahja](https://github.com/babunidev/Morkahja) is authoritative for actual encounter texts, IDs, ordered follow-links, avatar metadata and artwork files.
- This repository is a **novel adaptation**; new plot, dialogue, chronology, character biography and outcomes are fiction proposals, not silent additions to game canon.
- If sources disagree, record the tension; never smooth away contradictions without approval. Earlier chat summaries are not independently verified sources.

## Creative and editorial rules

Account for every source encounter by ID; a catalog row alone is not manuscript integration. An encounter can be directly experienced, retold, sung, remembered, witnessed, recorded or woven into another character's story. The original game choice, roll, reward or success condition need not be recreated. Preserve explicit encounter sequences and significant clues unless literary restructuring is documented.

Write dreamlike but grounded fantasy with attentive rhythm, senses and consequence. Distinguish ordinary warmth from mythic terror; do not inflate every moment. Brunti is a living Grimnir of mineral, metal and crystal, not an armored human. A novel may portray characters' decisions and outcomes while respecting unresolved world mysteries.

Use `story/` for planning, `manuscript/chapters/` for prose, `production/` for audiobook workflows and `docs/` for evidence and decisions. Keep narration exports clean of editorial commentary. Avoid unnecessary software dependencies.

## Record-keeping

Follow `docs/DOCUMENTATION.md` for first-person creative project voice and accurate AI/model attribution. Distinguish **direction, proposal, approved decision, draft, reviewed manuscript, recorded audio and publication**. Update roadmap, development journal, decisions and changelog when appropriate; never claim tests, editorial approval or distribution that did not occur.

Protect contributors' credits, personal data, credentials and rights. Link external art at first; do not copy unrelated private data, commissioned music or third-party audio into this public repository.

## Human–AI collaboration record

`docs/AI_COLLABORATION.md` is the short record of how Babuni works with AI and who did what, with the same text in all of Babuni's repositories. Do not add dated entries, session notes or per-change attribution to it. Record each contribution in `docs/DEVLOG.md` (what Babuni asked for and what the AI did, naming the tool and model) and in `CHANGELOG.md`. Change `docs/AI_COLLABORATION.md` only to add a row to its "Who did what" table when a new AI tool or model starts contributing to this project, or when Babuni revises the shared text.
