# Causal chain — thirty chapters

**Status:** Working **chapter causality design**, complemented by [109 scene briefs](scenes/README.md). This is not a finalized 30-chapter manuscript or independent editorial approval of every scene assignment. This connects the first 30-chapter [outline](OUTLINE_V1.md) to the [story spine](STORY_SPINE.md) and [203-encounter matrix](PLACEMENT_MATRIX.md). The scene groups and game IDs from v1 are retained. These are intended dramatic links, not canon facts.

A strong chapter should change what the next chapter can happen *because of*, even if some time is allowed for beauty, rest and a voice heard in passing. Do not manufacture a clue in every encounter.

## Movement I — Stone and Departure

| Chapter | Turn of action and character | Consequence that carries forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Skyland Morning | Brunti braces a strangely dissonant memorial assembly too quickly. The support transfers its double resonance into a resting room. She stops it and admits her part. | Darrik isolates the panel; a safe repair deadline is set. She must choose between waiting and investigating. |
| 2. The Weight of Other Stones | Testimony of wounded Grimnir and rebels makes clear that preserving a history is not the same as correcting someone's story. Brunti accepts a council-sanctioned search for old repair provenance. | Her inquiry becomes a real task, with a return obligation rather than a private quest for a prophecy. |
| 3. A Road Made of Grass | The Earthsinger asks for expression rather than force amid meadows whose sounds differ. Brunti cannot make a single rule fit them all. | She begins recording signs she can describe without already interpreting them. |
| 4. Letters Without a Word | Seven carved letters and an unrelated cube tempt Brunti to invent a message. Sira's Bloomwalker song resists the neat reading; the two argue and then agree to travel together toward Aetherion. | A companion with independent obligations begins to interrupt the self-contained investigation. |

## Chronology and character presence

- **Brunti** carries the close third-person line throughout; a character's testimony may briefly widen it only with a clear speaker and occasion.
- **Darrik** is present at the beginning and return; letters or messages during the journey should be sparse, non-magical unless supported by source craft.
- **Sira** joins ch. 4, travels through the Vale/Evergrove and Karrhu, pursues aid commitments separately in ch. 16–20, and reunites for Keldor's rescue. No unexplained instant travel is assumed; its exact timing and routes remain to be checked.
- **Velen** joins at ch. 11, travels to Everwinter and Keldor's perimeter, and does **not** enter Blackwood. Their records are an independent check, not omniscient narration.
- Aetherion, Evergrove, Ohgar, Karrhu, Everwinter and Keldor are **not claimed to be neighboring places**. Routes, distances and dates need further source verification. Distant reports do not entail physical travel.
- Every source encounter retains its proposed primary placement in [PLACEMENT_MATRIX.md](PLACEMENT_MATRIX.md). This chain does not certify 203 finished scene integrations. The three short follow-pairs, seven engraved letters and Blackwood order must be preserved during subsequent scene planning.

## Scenes requiring special scrutiny

**Ch. 4 — clue letters:** Keep A H K J O M A intact and **not decoded**. Brunti's personal misreading must be hers, not a false claim that the game contains a universal password.

**Ch. 13 → 23 — displaced child's identity:** A relationship between the Evergrove raid survivors and a particular Keldor child is **invented for this novel**. Preserve the separate game identities of Meadowheart Lostling (20169), Forestwander Sproutling (20170) and Fringebound Escapee (20171); do not merge them. Only the child with the walking stick is considered for the displacement arc, subject to later source and geography validation.

**Ch. 25 — portal access:** No established key or reliable activation method. Narrate the appearance and passage without assigning causation to the memorial stones, the letter clues, Brunti's ancestry or a magic command.

**Ch. 27 — recovery:** Do not turn the Verdani healer into an expositor proving a buried god exists.

**Ch. 28 — Atranor:** The realm is canonically real, but a mortal report's provenance is uncertain. Do not invent a certain future, automatic resurrection or return from reincarnation with memory intact.

## Stage 2 revised-prose causal notes (18 November 2026)

This planning document's original 30-chapter table is retained as its historical working design. [Stage 2's edited manuscript and evidence](../docs/REVISION_STAGE_2_REVIEW.md) refine several linked turns:

- **Ch1–2:** terrace watch training gives Brunti practical protective skill, making her failure to account for an unseen inhabited chamber a consequential mistake. The local council projects a **roughly six-month** convergence window.
- **Ch11 → Ch20:** the fear of facing **her own** community with an incomplete answer persists despite Brunti's developing evidence discipline. Months pass in ordinary travel; the fourth month is the explicit Ch20 anchor.
- **Ch20 → Ch23:** a real missing-child lead justifies continued travel, but Ch23 establishes a **safe, timely wagon** home after the rescue.
- **Ch23 → Ch24:** Brunti declares they should leave, then **knowingly gives up her held seat** to seek an unnecessary Blackwood revelation. She forfeits companionship and reliable arrival before convergence, not her ability to help a trapped child (already rescued).
- **Ch25–27:** Blackwood remains a disorienting, ordered game encounter sequence. Two wardens find Brunti **outside the unknown inner passage** at an exterior boundary marker; an ordinary litter, ridge post, wagon and station message explain access to care and Sira's whereabouts, not the uncanny escape.
- **Ch28 → Ch29:** Darrik's dated notice narrows the convergence window; ordinary staffed travel returns Brunti with an injured wrist. Her truthful council testimony states **both** the physical evidence and her personal cowardice in delaying return. Actual memorial work waits for the convergence materials and access established in Stage 1.

This is a **revision note**, not a claim that the source encounters were rechecked one by one or that new fiction has entered MorkahjaWorld canon. Stage 3's full literary rhythm, scene function and repetition pass remains outstanding.
