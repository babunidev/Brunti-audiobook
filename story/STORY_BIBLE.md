# Brunti — working story bible

**Status: unapproved literary proposal.** The source roots below are canonical; the plot direction is not.

## Narrative promise

A young woman of living stone leaves the certainty of the moving Skylands and discovers that Morkahja is kept together as much by what people remember and tell one another as by the things they build. She can meet danger with weapons, but knowledge, care and unanswered questions require different courage.

This is not a game transcript. Brunti need not attend every source encounter. Her view can broaden through a merchant's account, an archive's record, a keeper's testimony, a song, a remembered encounter or the lives of other travelers.

## Established world foundation

The [Grimnir](../docs/SOURCES.md) are mineral-and-metal beings whose bodies have crystalline structure and whose cultural world embraces geomancy, resonance, communal craft and engineered **Skylands**. Birth takes place through communal care around a resonance bath, not ordinary human biology. Food, prepared minerals and resonance sustain their bodies. Extended elemental isolation deep underground can cause a particular Stonebound condition, but ordinary cave visits do not immediately cause it.

Keldor's corruption and the Blackwood Abyss remain mysteries with multiple in-world theories. The afterlife of Atranor, resurrection and reincarnation operate under shared divine laws; no god knows unwritten futures. Avoid "solving" these questions without a creative decision.

## Possible inciting event — **proposal**

A memory-stone in Brunti's Skyland begins to sound a broken rhythm no craftsperson recognizes. Whether it is a damaged recollection, a distant call or a false trace is unknown. The event might lead Brunti toward Aetherion's archives, northern witnesses and the unsettling accounts of Keldor.

**This anomaly is invented as a literary possibility. It is not a documented event in Morkahja's canon.**

## Proposed emotional and thematic direction

Brunti is competent with her hands and direct in a fight, but less certain when testimony disagrees or an enemy is also a person in need. The adventure may challenge the idea that every danger must be defeated and every mystery exposed. Resonance, memory, movement, listening, hospitality and thresholds can connect otherwise distant encounters.

## Unresolved

The protagonist's family, exact age, defining relationships, companion characters, antagonist, chronology, actual inciting incident, climax, ending and final title remain undecided. The five movements in [STRUCTURE](STRUCTURE.md) are a scaffold, not a committed outline.
