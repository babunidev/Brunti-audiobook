# Source-reading survey — first literary pass

**Source snapshot:** [Morkahja encounters 58576f063627](https://github.com/babunidev/Morkahja/blob/58576f063627752f77cdf99514baad875d509114/server/game/encountersdata.json). **Status:** first scene-level review of the 203 encounter records, with targeted reading of relevant world entries. This is **not** a complete paragraph-by-paragraph close edit or approval of the resulting fiction.

The original catalog contains more than fights. Meadows, pillars, brooks and shelter places often distinguish themselves by tiny sounds or changes in light. A faithful novel must preserve their separate textures rather than transform each into another treasure hunt. Injured Grimnir, drained Aevis, hungry Verdani children, spent Rokur and a wounded spirit stag carry moments of **need before choice**. The mother's protective posture and the young Redeye's play similarly interrupt the assumption that every animal encounter demands violence.

Aetherion gives a narrative device grounded in the world: an Archive where books speak with their authors' voices, a forge, harmonic concert and elemental garden that seems out of rhythm. **No source establishes a causal connection** between that irregularity and a newly invented Skyland memory-stone incident. In Everwinter, the trading post, cave, preserved Frostweaver history, Kaldric, Lyria, Jorvald and the Seer offer different types of witness rather than one explanatory oracle.

Keldor requires modulation. Its dubious merchants, living scholars, defending Aevis, young Verdani and exhausted Rokur are important precisely because they survive amid corruption. Dark creatures do not all have an established common controller. The eight-encounter Blackwood descent is structurally exceptional: portal, tangled roots, guardian, singing faces, anomalous reflections, procession, throne and healer's vigil form an ordered lived sequence.

## Canon cautions for later close edit

- Grimnir physiology is living mineral/metal/crystal; older prose sometimes has conventional flesh, sweat or blood and should not establish human biology for Brunti.
- [Evergrove](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Evergrove.md) is a desert heartland with Verdani oasis-gardens; [Celesterra](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Celesterra.md) is an Aevis subterranean sky-city, not a Grimnir homeland.
- [The Urcogor Ravage](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/WorldEvents/Urcogor_Ravage.md) recurs with no settled origin; related peoples cannot be uniformly assigned evil intent.
- [Keldor and Blackwood](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Blackwood_Abyss.md) carry deliberately competing accounts; do not close their core mysteries.
- [Divine Laws](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Pantheon/Divine_Laws.md) govern Atranor, resurrection and reincarnation; no deity knows the unwritten future.
- The seven engraved letters remain A H K J O M A in ID order; neither the game data nor the index establishes a decoded meaning.
- **Original game outcomes are not the novel's predetermined outcomes.** The writer must invent consequences transparently within the novel, without upgrading those inventions into world lore.

The [full 203-record proposal](PLACEMENT_MATRIX.md) is a planning tool. The next editorial gate is a paragraph-by-paragraph check of each chapter's source prose and relevant lore before a final beat sheet or manuscript draft is approved.
