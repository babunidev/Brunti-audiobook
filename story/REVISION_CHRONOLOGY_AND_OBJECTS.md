### Chronology blockers for Stage 1–2

- **T1 — Convergence:** No finite date/window can yet be calculated. Early chapters demand convergence-only materials/access; Chapter 29 performs final work while it is still approaching. Chapter 30's “next convergence” line must be inspected after selecting a repair timing.
- **T2 — Overall duration:** “Several weeks” at Chapter 11 and “months” by Chapters 22 and 29 are clear relative anchors; the full time between convergence announcement, northern journey, rescue, Blackwood injury and return is **not** calculable from text. Do not repair by inventing a date in this ledger.
- **T3 — Weather and seasons:** Chapter 10 calls the Vale summer; Chapter 17 explicitly uses winter sun/conditions farther north, and Chapter 21 retains Everwinter cold; the interval and latitudinal differences should be validated against source geography before implying a global seasonal transition.
- **T4 — Companions:** Sira: meets Brunti in Ch4; travels through Ch16; westward leave Ch16; reunites Ch21; departs shelter Ch24; visits lazaret Ch27; travels in Ch28 but does **not** ascend home with Brunti. Velen: joins Ch11 with leave approved Ch12; stays with Brunti through Ch23; travels with records Ch24; visits lazaret Ch27; accompanies Brunti to Skyland Ch28–29.
- **T5 — Travel source scope:** Frostwind/Everwinter and Keldor are actually traversed; northern container reports in Ch7 and the twelve corruption records in Ch16 are **Archive**, not teleportation. Keep testimony and geography distinct.

## D. Keepsake, injury and recovery continuity

- **Nemi's gift:** [Ch2](../manuscript/chapters/02-the-weight-of-other-stones.md) promises a small item from below; [Ch3](../manuscript/chapters/03-a-road-made-of-grass.md) Brunti receives Earthsinger's small dark seam-streaked pebble; [Ch5](../manuscript/chapters/05-the-lives-of-the-vale.md) she does not give it away; [Ch16](../manuscript/chapters/16-witnesses-of-corruption.md) explicitly says she still carries it **for Nemi**. [Ch29–30](../manuscript/chapters/30-the-note-that-remains.md) depict Nemi but **not its handover or loss**. Ledger state: **open**, not magically rewarded.
- **Damaged hand/wrist:** Frostwind/Everwinter cold and repairs affect Brunti's mineral joints ([Ch17–20](../manuscript/chapters/20-the-broken-frostweaver-record.md)); after Blackwood her **left wrist is fractured and braced** ([Ch27](../manuscript/chapters/27-what-the-throne-keeps.md)), and remains restricted during return and Skyland craft ([Ch28–30](../manuscript/chapters/30-the-note-that-remains.md)). Do not imply instant normal strength or human flesh healing.
- **Blackwood recovery:** Brunti collapses at Silent Throne and next remembers the lazaret; healer says she was recovered from **deep unsafe Keldor** but cannot name discovery/transport chain or exact elapsed days ([Ch27](../manuscript/chapters/27-what-the-throne-keeps.md)). Ledger state: **no ordinary recovery bridge established**. A future proposal can explain discovery outside the abyss without explaining the abyss.
- **The Verdani girl and other travelers:** [Ch23](../manuscript/chapters/23-those-who-still-tend-the-forest.md) identifies and shelters the girl after multiple independent witness checks, with the other boy and injured woman remaining distinct. [Ch28](../manuscript/chapters/28-a-story-about-the-end.md) states the girl traveled onward but that family reunion **had not been confirmed** in the last report.
- **The two memorial stones:** physical identities remain unknown until the casing opens in Ch29 as intended; all earlier properly verified similarities are anonymous. The names in Ch25–28 are a **reader-facing draft contradiction**, not deliberate prophecy.
