### Chronology blockers for Stage 1–2

- **T1 — Convergence:** No finite date/window can yet be calculated. Early chapters demand convergence-only materials/access; Chapter 29 performs final work while it is still approaching. Chapter 30's “next convergence” line must be inspected after selecting a repair timing.
- **T2 — Overall duration:** “Several weeks” at Chapter 11 and “months” by Chapters 22 and 29 are clear relative anchors; the full time between convergence announcement, northern journey, rescue, Blackwood injury and return is **not** calculable from text. Do not repair by inventing a date in this ledger.
- **T3 — Weather and seasons:** Chapter 10 calls the Vale summer; Chapter 17 explicitly uses winter sun/conditions farther north, and Chapter 21 retains Everwinter cold; the interval and latitudinal differences should be validated against source geography before implying a global seasonal transition.
- **T4 — Companions:** Sira: meets Brunti in Ch4; travels through Ch16; westward leave Ch16; reunites Ch21; departs shelter Ch24; visits lazaret Ch27; travels in Ch28 but does **not** ascend home with Brunti. Velen: joins Ch11 with leave approved Ch12; stays with Brunti through Ch23; travels with records Ch24; visits lazaret Ch27; accompanies Brunti to Skyland Ch28–29.
- **T5 — Travel source scope:** Frostwind/Everwinter and Keldor are actually traversed; northern container reports in Ch7 and the twelve corruption records in Ch16 are **Archive**, not teleportation. Keep testimony and geography distinct.
