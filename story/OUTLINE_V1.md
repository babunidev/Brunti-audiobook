# Brunti — thirty-chapter plot architecture, proposal v1

**Status:** Original source-placement outline **v1**, kept for historical traceability. Its working plot is superseded and refined by the [story spine](STORY_SPINE.md), [character arcs](CHARACTER_ARCS.md) and [30-chapter causal chain](CAUSAL_CHAIN.md). This remains a chapter inventory, not manuscript prose. The chapter count, story premise, supporting characters, chronology and ending are all open to creative revision. The narrative must never contradict established [MorkahjaWorld](../docs/SOURCES.md) lore.

## Premise

A young Grimnir warrior lives among the moving Skylands, where community and ancestral memory are woven into crafted stone. A communal memory stone begins sounding an unfamiliar rhythm. **This is a fictional framing incident, not existing Morkahja canon.** Brunti travels not to slay its mysterious author but to seek witnesses, compare accounts and learn what can truly be preserved.

Her journey widens: the Vale reveals ordinary beauty and threatened lives; Aetherion can hold speaking archives and the Garden of Elements' own unexplained irregularity; the Ravage's aftermath shows loss without a tidy villain; desert, swamp and frozen country carry different traditions. The source's Blackwood descent gives a dramatic culmination without claiming to solve the forest's history.

The possible ending is an ethical choice: Brunti returns with incompatible stories and **refuses to fuse them into a false single truth**. That decision can change communal practice even if the stone's strange rhythm, Keldor's cause and the elemental irregularities remain unresolved. Physical strength remains useful, but it cannot settle testimony.

## Five movements and 30 chapter briefs

## Movement I — Stone and Departure

### Chapter 1 — Skyland Morning

**Place/form:** Grimnir Skylands. **Source IDs:** Original connecting fiction; no primary encounter ID.

A communal memory stone develops an unfamiliar rhythm. Establish Brunti through craft and relationships, not an announced prophecy.


### Chapter 2 — The Weight of Other Stones

**Place/form:** Skyland meeting; testimonies from below. **Source IDs:** 20015, 20018, 20055, 20056, 20128.

Witnesses bring news of damaged Grimnir lives, an elder's hunger and rebel isolation. Brunti learns that community must carry more than victories.


### Chapter 3 — A Road Made of Grass

**Place/form:** Ethera's Vale. **Source IDs:** 20001, 20002, 20003, 20004, 20005, 20069, 20070, 20071, 20072, 20080, 20185.

The first road is gentle but not empty; meadows sound and change differently, and an Earthsinger meets strength with an invitation.


### Chapter 4 — Letters Without a Word

**Place/form:** Vale meadows. **Source IDs:** 20074, 20081, 20083, 20084, 20085, 20086, 20087, 20088, 20089, 20090, 20186.

Seven inscriptions form A H K J O M A in encounter order. Preserve their uncertainty while a Bloomwalker offers song rather than interpretation.


## Movement II — Many Voices

### Chapter 5 — The Lives of the Vale

**Place/form:** Vale wilderness. **Source IDs:** 20032, 20033, 20034, 20051, 20052, 20065, 20067, 20091, 20092, 20093, 20094, 20095, 20096, 20097, 20110, 20111.

A living ecology of boars, wolves and other creatures: vary danger, observation and campfire testimony instead of staging continuous fights.


### Chapter 6 — Under Eldertree

**Place/form:** Eldertree Forest. **Source IDs:** 20006, 20007, 20011, 20039, 20040, 20041, 20042, 20043.

Aevis works, a magic source and shaded refuges let Brunti encounter an older world that does not require a conqueror.


### Chapter 7 — Things That Are Kept

**Place/form:** Eldertree and woodland. **Source IDs:** 20008, 20009, 20010, 20045, 20046, 20047, 20048, 20049, 20068, 20073, 20098, 20119.

Recovered chests, Grimnir steps and quiet shelters become a discussion about the uses and ethics of keeping objects.


### Chapter 8 — At the Green Boundary

**Place/form:** Liani Forest edge. **Source IDs:** 20036, 20114, 20115, 20117.

Liani and winged creatures mark a boundary that Brunti cannot own; seeing without striking becomes an important skill.


### Chapter 9 — Where Stories Are Sold

**Place/form:** Trade roads and Eldridia. **Source IDs:** 20012, 20031, 20059, 20060, 20076.

Markets, a Pantheon temple, a weary smith and an Aevis storyteller show how a story gains and loses meaning in trade.


### Chapter 10 — The Shelter Beyond the Veil

**Place/form:** Ethera's Vale roads. **Source IDs:** 19444, 20187, 20188, 20189, 20190, 20191.

Aid for weary pilgrims and a threatened Aevis leads to the linked hearth seal and its refuge; even the sleeping Ogru becomes a question of restraint.


### Chapter 11 — The City of Listening

**Place/form:** Aetherion. **Source IDs:** 20062, 20181, 20182, 20183, 20184.

A speaking archive, elemental concert, forge and failing garden suggest competing kinds of evidence; the city's irregularity is not proved to share a source with Brunti's home.


### Chapter 12 — Where Water Is Guarded

**Place/form:** Evergrove and southeastern Vale. **Source IDs:** 19001, 20064, 20192, 20193.

Quiet worship at Umbrael's altar contrasts with Nereval's guarded sanctuary; the nearby riverkeeper offers humor and human scale.


## Movement III — Land and Loss

### Chapter 13 — After the Raid

**Place/form:** Evergrove; distant reports. **Source IDs:** 20013, 20014, 20016, 20017, 20053, 20054, 20058, 20125, 20126, 20127.

Exhaustion, lost homes, survivors rebuilding and the aftermath of the recurring Ravage demand attention to distinct lives.


### Chapter 14 — Words in the Sand

**Place/form:** Ohgar. **Source IDs:** 20035, 20063, 20066, 20101.

Water, heat and an inscribed temple disc challenge Brunti's assumptions about reading stone and understanding a stranger's needs.


### Chapter 15 — Beneath the Reeds

**Place/form:** Karrhu. **Source IDs:** 20037, 20038, 20057, 20077, 20082, 20116.

A hungry Verdani child, a silver pool and dangerous living creatures make the swamp a place of care and caution.


### Chapter 16 — Witnesses of Corruption

**Place/form:** Collected regional accounts. **Source IDs:** 20019, 20020, 20021, 20022, 20023, 20024, 20025, 20026, 20027, 20028, 20029, 20030.

Separate records show corrupted beings across varied lands; do not claim they share one commander or that all stood before Brunti.


### Chapter 17 — The Mountain Has a Voice

**Place/form:** Frostwind Mountains. **Source IDs:** 20061, 20099, 20100, 20102, 20103, 20105, 20106, 20109, 20120, 20121, 20122.

A dealer, singing cliffs, a dangerous pass and mountain animals build bodily hardship before Everwinter's deeper stories.


### Chapter 18 — The Last Warm Bowl

**Place/form:** Everwinter outskirts. **Source IDs:** 20104, 20123, 20124, 20132, 20176.

A trading post and the hungry, cold living world offer warmth, companionship, rockfall and animal danger without a trophy hunt.


### Chapter 19 — Ice That Remembers

**Place/form:** Everwinter Valley. **Source IDs:** 20129, 20130, 20131, 20135, 20136, 20140, 20141.

A guardian and cave form a linked passage. Preserved lives, uncertain omens and an inviting but dangerous spirit challenge simple memory.


### Chapter 20 — The Broken Frostweaver Record

**Place/form:** Frostweaver ruins and Skyreach. **Source IDs:** 20133, 20134, 20137, 20138, 20139, 20142, 20143, 20144, 20145.

Kaldric, Lyria, the Seer and Jorvald hold partial accounts; the seal and vault must not yield a neat universal explanation.


## Movement IV — The Blackwood Descent

### Chapter 21 — Before the Cursed Trees

**Place/form:** Vale–Keldor border. **Source IDs:** 20044, 20107, 20108, 20112, 20113.

Altered wildlife makes the border tangible; strength no longer suffices to distinguish a threat from a warning.


### Chapter 22 — The Price of Warning

**Place/form:** Keldor outer forest. **Source IDs:** 20075, 20078, 20079, 20118, 20155.

A dubious merchant, cursed Gobbls and watch-birds illustrate the varied bargains and hazards of the forest.


### Chapter 23 — Those Who Still Tend the Forest

**Place/form:** Keldor clearings. **Source IDs:** 20148, 20162, 20167, 20168, 20169, 20170, 20171, 20172, 20173, 20174, 20175, 20177.

Healers, lost children, defenders, scholars and merchants persist in a cursed place. Brunti's task becomes practical protection.


### Chapter 24 — Paths Among the Dead

**Place/form:** Deeper Keldor. **Source IDs:** 20146, 20147, 20149, 20150, 20151, 20152, 20153, 20156, 20157, 20158, 20159, 20160, 20161, 20163, 20164, 20165, 20166, 20178, 20179, 20180.

Corrupted roots, dead paths, monstrous presences and sealed objects make navigation itself an ordeal.


### Chapter 25 — The Door in the Cliff

**Place/form:** Blackwood Portal and outer Abyss. **Source IDs:** 20050, 19666, 19667.

Keep the portal, Tangled Depths and Guardian in the canonical follow order. Do not invent a confirmed portal key.


### Chapter 26 — The Voices Beneath

**Place/form:** Blackwood Abyss. **Source IDs:** 19668, 19669, 19670.

The Hollow Choir, Weeping Mire and Forgotten Procession are one deepening sequence of unreliable reflections and memory.


### Chapter 27 — What the Throne Keeps

**Place/form:** Silent Throne and Keldor fringe. **Source IDs:** 19671, 19672.

The Silent Throne is immediately followed by Verdani's Vigil; the return does not verify every perception.


## Movement V — The Returning Note

### Chapter 28 — A Story About the End

**Place/form:** Road home; distant testimony. **Source IDs:** 1.

An uncertain account of Atranor frames the final moral decisions without forcing Brunti to die or claiming divine certainty.


### Chapter 29 — The Work of Bringing Back

**Place/form:** Grimnir Skylands. **Source IDs:** Original connecting fiction; no primary encounter ID.

The gathered accounts reach a living community. The proposed personal climax is Brunti's refusal to forge incompatible stories into one false truth.


### Chapter 30 — The Note That Remains

**Place/form:** Grimnir Skylands. **Source IDs:** 2.

A quiet coda reveals growth through action instead of a literal level-up, and leaves the wider world alive.



## Dramatic relationships and limits

Brunti begins with confidence in craft and martial defense. The journey confronts her with lives that require food, listening, protection of a sanctuary, and the right to leave a mystery unresolved. **Historical v1 note:** At the time of this outline no companions had been chosen; the [current cast](CHARACTER_ARCS.md) now selects a working company for the audiobook. Source figures—Frostbite Fergus, Jorvald, Kaldric, Lyria, Grimnir rebels, Verdani healers and travelers—retain their source roles; additional biographies or changed actions are fictional proposals only.

The notional route is Skylands → Vale/Eldertree → Aetherion and Evergrove → accounts from elsewhere → Ohgar/Karrhu → Frostwind/Everwinter → Keldor → Blackwood → return. **No travel times, fixed roads or teleports are asserted.** Testimony and archives deliberately carry geographically remote events without forcing Brunti to visit every source location.

An audiobook version should mark speakers clearly and make archive voices or extended testimony dramatic rather than recited inventory. Avoid pretending original game pre-decision passages specify consequences. Mystery is not a license for an arbitrary explanation.
