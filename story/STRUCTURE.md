# Story structure — working plan

The earlier five-movement sketch has developed into a **proposed, unapproved 30-chapter architecture**. The definitive working references are [Outline v1](OUTLINE_V1.md), the [203-encounter placement matrix](PLACEMENT_MATRIX.md) and the [scene-level literary survey](LITERARY_SURVEY.md).

| Movement | Chapters | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I — Stone and Departure | 1–4 | Grimnir identity, need for witnesses, arrival in the Vale, inscribed-letter trail |
| II — Many Voices | 5–12 | Ecosystem, care, trade, Aetherion, worship, regional testimony |
| III — Land and Loss | 13–20 | Ravage consequences, Ohgar, Karrhu, corruption reports, Everwinter |
| IV — The Blackwood Descent | 21–27 | Keldor's threatened inhabitants, descent, Silent Throne, healer's vigil |
| V — The Returning Note | 28–30 | Mortality, an ethical return to community, earned personal growth |

**Status:** 203 unique primary placements are **proposals**. No scene brief is approved, no manuscript chapter exists, and the source atlas intentionally still calls each original record Unplaced in the *approved/drafted* sense. Proposed geography and causal links have not undergone a full lore-by-lore continuity audit.
