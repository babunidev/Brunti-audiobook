# Provisional narrative movements

**Planning sketch only — not approved chapters or encounter assignments.** All 203 atlas rows remain unplaced.

## I — The moving home

Brunti's social world among the Grimnir Skylands. Establish ordinary life, work, friendship, the sensory experience of resonance and the possible first disturbance. **Function:** give the adventure emotional stakes before it becomes travel.

## II — Voices along the roads

Morkahja opens through landscapes, travelers, markets, healers and small dangers. Gather experiences that need not be Brunti's own. Aetherion is a potential nexus for records and reports. **Function:** widen the world without stringing together random battles.

## III — Accounts from the cold

Everwinter's people, ruins, guardians and losses may allow a substantially different regional voice, through first-hand travel or a character's extended testimony. **Function:** make memory, cost and endurance concrete.

## IV — The forest that remembers

Keldor and the explicit Blackwood follow-chain provide a strong long-form sequence: portal, descent, uncanny witnesses, Silent Throne and Verdani's Vigil. **Function:** confront the difference between learning a mystery and claiming to own its explanation.

## V — What may be carried home

A conclusion could return to Grimnir identity, communal life and a changed relationship to the stories Brunti heard. Atranor and divine themes must respect the canon of mortality and reincarnation; no resurrection or verdict is implied by this sketch. **Function:** resolve the protagonist's personal arc without declaring answers to every world mystery.

## Structural requirements for the next stage

Assign specific **encounter IDs**, chronology, regions, storytellers and plot consequences after individual reading. The game expansion buckets must not be treated as a rigid geographical route. Determine what Brunti wants, what she risks, who stands alongside her, what changes, and how the ending earns its final note before fixing chapter titles or word counts.
