## Chapter 1 — Skyland Morning

**Opening:** At the inner wall of a moving Skyland, an everyday repair becomes audible before it becomes dangerous.

**Present / framing voices:** Brunti; Darrik; unnamed chamber residents; a craft council.

#### 01.1 — The untroubled morning

Show workshops, listening stones, mineral food and the small pride Brunti takes in helping others. Her skill is tangible before her flaw is visible.

**Turn:** A second beat enters the memorial wall's familiar resonance.

**Source encounters:** Original linking fiction; no source encounter ID.

#### 01.2 — The brace

Brunti adds a metal support before checking the neighboring resonance path. The unfamiliar beat travels into a resting chamber, waking a fragile resident from repair-rest.

**Turn:** She removes the pressure; immediate safety takes precedence over saving face.

**Source encounters:** Original linking fiction; no source encounter ID.

#### 01.3 — The report

Darrik isolates the panel and asks what Brunti actually touched. She admits the full sequence. He sets a safe transfer plan; she argues the old joint deserves examination.

**Turn:** The council allows a time-bounded search for provenance while Darrik safeguards the home.

**Source encounters:** Original linking fiction; no source encounter ID.

**Chapter handoff:** The Skyland continues moving. Brunti leaves with a drawing of an unlabeled support, a practical deadline and no hint of destiny.

## Chapter 2 — The Weight of Other Stones

**Opening:** The council hears reports from the lands below while deciding how much of its own history it can responsibly keep in active architecture.

**Present / framing voices:** Brunti; Darrik; Grimnir Skykeepers and travelers.

#### 02.1 — Wounds have names

Three separate testimonies distinguish a kneeling cave warrior, Stonefist at the ruined temple, and a woman hurt in a Crumblegheist fight. Neither the injuries nor their locations are combined into one attack.

**Turn:** Brunti hears how different people describe the same condition—being unable to carry one's usual weight.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20015 — Weakened Grimnir** (*Testimony*): cave warrior wounded.
  - **20018 — Stonefist Ironwill** (*Testimony*): Stonefist resting in temple ruins.
  - **20055 — Wounded Grimnir** (*Testimony*): Grimnir injured by Crumblegheist.

#### 02.2 — A bowl and a fire

A courier describes an elderly Grimnir after a raid and a separate Stoneguard rebel refuge. Their political aims remain unknown. A witness corrects Brunti when she calls their loss a problem with one solution.

**Turn:** She agrees to record what is known, including the gaps.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20056 — Venerable Grimnir** (*Testimony*): hungry elder after Ravage.
  - **20128 — Grimnir Rebels** (*Testimony*): Stoneguard rebels by dim fire.

#### 02.3 — What may travel

Darrik delegates no magical cure. The council grants Brunti access to an old transport ledger and permits ordinary travel to seek restoration evidence; Darrik's protective work continues without her.

**Turn:** She chooses a witness's route toward the Vale and the civic archive, not an arbitrary pilgrimage.

**Source encounters:** Original linking fiction; no source encounter ID.

**Chapter handoff:** The task is bounded: find records of how the composite memorial came to be, not solve every injury in Morkahja.

## Chapter 3 — A Road Made of Grass

**Opening:** Descending by an established trade visit from the moving homeland, Brunti feels the ground no longer answering in her Skyland's measured rhythms.

**Present / framing voices:** Brunti; roadside guides; Earthsinger.

#### 03.1 — Stones in the meadow

Grass conceals small shapes; a stream passes worn pillars; a laugh seems to hang between Fae Pastures stones. Let the wind and shade provide the first three distinct experiences.

**Turn:** Brunti initially records them all as instances of one unknown signal.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20001 — Lovely Meadows** (*Lived*): mossy meadow stones.
  - **20002 — Enchanted Glades** (*Lived*): stream and worn pillars.
  - **20003 — Fae Pastures** (*Lived*): strange laughter between pillars.

#### 03.2 — Five different silences

A summit pillar bends the grass into circles; plains pillars hum faintly; flowers, a butterfly and a pink valley passage supply intervals of ordinary beauty. No secret mechanism is inferred.

**Turn:** She learns to give each place its own descriptive line.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20004 — Mystic Grass** (*Lived*): grass circles summit stone.
  - **20005 — Seraphic Plains** (*Lived*): hollow sound between monoliths.
  - **20069 — Celestial Fields** (*Lived*): wildflowers and a butterfly.
  - **20072 — Tranquil Wilds** (*Lived*): golden grass and falling leaf.
  - **20080 — Dreamy Valley** (*Lived*): pink flowers between rocks.

#### 03.3 — A false pattern

A melodious hollow with still flowers and a luminous stone is distinct from another meadow's artifact rumor. Compare a specific observation to an unverified report.

**Turn:** Brunti strikes out her speculative claim that both must concern her memorial.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20070 — Enigmatic Prairies** (*Testimony*): faint notes draw still flowers toward luminous stone.
  - **20071 — Arcane Meadows** (*Testimony*): distinct meadow treasure rumor.

#### 03.4 — The Earthsinger

A pilgrim challenges Brunti to answer his staff's tone with an act of craft or motion instead of trying to silence it. The moment can be playful and unresolved.

**Turn:** A practiced warrior chooses to listen twice before responding.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20185 — Earthsinger's Challenge** (*Lived*): Earthsinger offers challenge.

**Chapter handoff:** The first pages of her journey book are messy but more truthful than her initial confident conclusions.

## Chapter 4 — Letters Without a Word

**Opening:** Across successive Vale travel days, different marked stones appear; their resemblance is too strong to ignore and too incomplete to explain.

**Present / framing voices:** Brunti; the Verdani Bloomwalker later named Sira; passing travelers.

#### 04.1 — The first stones

An A under flowers, an H amid evening gardens and a K on a sunlit boulder are copied exactly. Time and place changes remain audible between finds.

**Turn:** Brunti begins to see a code where only inscriptions are certain.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20081 — Arcadian Hills** (*Lived*): marked borderstone A.
  - **20083 — Enchanted Gardens** (*Lived*): garden engraving H.
  - **20084 — Mystical Vale** (*Lived*): vale boulder K.

#### 04.2 — The rest of the signs

A dale J, downs O, heath M and glen A appear amid flowers and shifting hillside light. The letters remain **A H K J O M A**, in source order; no invented translation.

**Turn:** Brunti has an accurate list but no authorized answer.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20085 — Bucolic Dale** (*Lived*): dale stone J.
  - **20086 — Captivating Downs** (*Lived*): downs carving O.
  - **20087 — Radiant Heath** (*Lived*): heath engraving M.
  - **20088 — Mesmerizing Hillsides** (*Lived*): hillsides turn between light and shade.
  - **20089 — Magical Glens** (*Lived*): glen rock A.

#### 04.3 — The cube is not a key

An etched cube pulses while flowers in another glade retain light under cloud shadow. Brunti nearly fuses them with the letter stones but cannot demonstrate a link.

**Turn:** A record can remain meaningful even when it answers nothing.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20074 — Mysterious Cube** (*Archive*): etched cube's pulses.
  - **20090 — Ethereal Glades** (*Lived*): flowers retain light under clouds.

#### 04.4 — Room for another step

A young Verdani in a blue robe, the Bloomwalker, invites company through a low song. Brunti asks whether it hides a code; Sira says a melody may simply be shared. Both are headed toward Aetherion.

**Turn:** Their disagreement, rather than a quest token, begins the companionship.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20186 — Melody's Bloomwalker** (*Lived*): Bloomwalker sings along path.

**Chapter handoff:** The chapter closes with two walkers taking different tempos on the same road.

**Writing gate:** Before drafting dialogue or chapter prose, check the entire source text for these IDs, ensure stated geography agrees with the world archive, and preserve the difference between a witnessed event and someone else's story.
