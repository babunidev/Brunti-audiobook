## How to convert a brief to novel prose

Each scene brief identifies its entrance, sensory material, immediate human-scale action, tension or choice, resulting turn, and source-specific encounter cues. Several scene cards include short testimonies or archives: those passages need a **speaker, occasion and emotional consequence** in the finished manuscript. Merely stating an encounter's name will not complete its integration.

The final novel should not sound like an encyclopedia or five separate adventure modules. Brunti's choices change the value of information, the company around her and her obligation to the people back home. Landscape, safe rest, odd humor and unsolved mysteries must remain part of the rhythm.
