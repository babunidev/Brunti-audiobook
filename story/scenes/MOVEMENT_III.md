# Movement III — Land and Loss: scene briefs v1

**Status:** working audiobook-fiction scene design; **Chapters 13–14 are now actual first-draft manuscript prose**; Chapters 15–20 remain planned. No canon changes. **Source baseline:** game encounter text `e826fa46d3ff` (203 source IDs), following first atlas and [reconciliation](../../docs/SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md). [Story spine](../STORY_SPINE.md) · [Matrix](../PLACEMENT_MATRIX.md) · [Continuity register](../../docs/SCENE_CONTINUITY.md).

The geography is intentionally **not** squeezed into one continuous afternoon. Evergrove is a Verdani desert/oasis heartland; Ohgar, Karrhu, Sarga and Frostwind are distinct lands whose exact routes and travel intervals require map checking. Corruption accounts are archive pieces, not twelve simultaneous fights.

## Chapter 13 — After the Raid

**Opening:** The first oasis-village is rebuilding: water has to be returned to its channels before anyone can sit and narrate the raid.

**Present / source voices:** Brunti; Sira; Velen; village survivors and correspondents.

#### 13.1 — The unfinished village

In an oasis-garden community damaged by **Ogru and Gobbl attackers**, survivors clear beams and repair channels. **Renn**, an exhausted male Verdani at the settlement's edge, first needs rest; **Alen** (local leader) and the villagers then choose the water-distribution priorities.

**Turn:** Sira chooses work over immediate story collecting; the company earns a place at the communal table.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20125 — Raided Verdani Village** (*Lived*): Verdani rebuild raided village.
  - **20053 — Exhausted Verdani** (*Lived*): spent Verdani at Evergrove edge.

#### 13.2 — Other voices of damage

Five **separate accounts**, gathered through caravan couriers, letters and travelers, evoke a scorched Rokur warrior, a spent **male** Aevis, a faded **female** Verdani in a mossy clearing, **Flameveil Emberheart** in a ruined *different* village, and a drained **female** Aevis in a woodland. Specific people and places stay distinct; none proves that their suffering belongs to the Evergrove raid.

**Turn:** Brunti stops making survivors into examples of the single event she hopes to explain.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20013 — Cauterized Rokur** (*Testimony*): charred Rokur after battle.
  - **20014 — Downed Aevis** (*Testimony*): exhausted Aevis beneath tree.
  - **20016 — Ailing Verdani** (*Testimony*): ailing Verdani in clearing.
  - **20017 — Flameveil Emberheart** (*Testimony*): Flameveil amid ruined home.
  - **20054 — Drained Aevis** (*Testimony*): drained Aevis in woodland.

#### 13.3 — A song still unfinished

An **elderly Aevis woman** hums an unfinished melody **directly in the cultivated oasis glade**, while separate **testimonies** convey a Rokur warrior's incomplete Battledance saga and the **Eternal Twilight Grove's** spirits beneath a starlit sky. The grove remains another place, not a new Evergrove region.

**Turn:** The company understands that silence can accompany grief without concealing a usable key.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20058 — Aevis Lady** (*Lived*): older Aevis with unfinished song.
  - **20126 — Disheartened Rokur** (*Testimony*): disheartened Rokur's broken saga.
  - **20127 — Eternal Twilight Grove** (*Testimony*): spirit grove's many laments.

#### 13.4 — The caravan that left

Sira hears that an **earlier displaced group** traveled by ordinary oasis caravans, including a child with a carved walking stick. A split caravan route leaves the child's whereabouts **unconfirmed, not yet an established disappearance**; nobody claims the child has reached Keldor. The latest scene sends **Brunti and Velen temporarily to a nearby supply station** for replacement pipe while Sira stays to help **Alen**; Chapter 14 must show their reunion before any three-person onward journey.

**Turn:** Sira retains a responsibility separate from Brunti's repair mission.

**Source encounters:** Original connecting fiction; no direct encounter ID.

**Chapter handoff:** Brunti and Velen depart **briefly for the next Oasis Heart** to fetch fitted water-channel pipe and records while Sira remains with the survivors. **Reunite them in the next chapter** before the onward desert crossing; the village's fourth channel and many homes remain unfinished.

## Chapter 14 — Words in the Sand

**Opening:** **Brunti and Velen first return from the nearest Oasis Heart to the raided Evergrove village** with correctly measured pipe, reunite with Sira, and reopen the fourth feeder under Alen/Renn's supervision. Only after several ordinary caravan legs and mapped wells do they reach Ohgar, where heat asks for care before interpretation.

**Present / source voices:** Brunti; Velen; Sira; desert guides and a young Grimnir traveler.

#### 14.1 — The sound of sand

**Sael**, an original desert guide, holds the caravan back from groaning granite monoliths where sand enters an unknown cleft, and later leads a detour around a dangerous **dark-brown armored Skorpo**. Pincers click and the venomous tail rises; no obligatory combat or treasure chamber is invented.

**Turn:** Brunti pays attention to thirst and shelter instead of treating every sound as code.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20101 — Rocks of Ohgar** (*Lived*): granite monoliths groan.
  - **20035 — Ohgar Skorpo** (*Lived*): desert scorpion's clicking pincers.

#### 14.2 — The young Grimnir

**Nera**, a novel-only young Grimnir woman, is found following shallow dragging footprints in open dunes, her exposed mineral surface dried and cracked. Brunti, Sira, Velen and Sael provide shade, gradual mineral-rich water and a supported transfer to a station for longer recovery, not an instantaneous cure.

**Turn:** Brunti's first instinct to solve can finally give way to an ordinary rescue.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20066 — Thirsty Grimnir** (*Lived*): thirsty Grimnir in open sun.

#### 14.3 — The great disc

The ruined temple's great upright disc, broken columns, sheltered runes and **two matching symbols flanking an erased section** remain partly unreadable. Velen and Brunti record the surviving strokes without filling gaps or claiming a link to Vale letters or Skyland. Brunti **sends Darrik an uncertain archive update**, with no guaranteed delivery and no request to delay the safe memorial transfer.

**Turn:** Brunti accepts that an inscription can be valuable even if it says nothing about her home.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20063 — Ancient Inscription** (*Lived*): weathered temple disc.

**Chapter handoff:** **All three companions** take mapped caravan/bridge routes toward Karrhu's wetland approaches, with weather and crossing conditions ahead. No instantaneous jump from Ohgar's desert to Karrhu's swamp; Nera remains resting at the Ohgar station and no post-rescue fate is falsely confirmed.

## Chapter 15 — Beneath the Reeds

**Opening:** In Karrhu the swamp answers every step with a sound that may belong to water or something alive beneath it.

**Present / source voices:** Brunti; Sira; Velen; a child living by swamp routes.

#### 15.1 — Beauty before teeth

The Inferno Duck's sudden heat is almost ridiculous; Aradia's silver wings remain dangerous. Allow humor to make caution feel lived rather than announced.

**Turn:** Sira takes the lead in deciding when they should withdraw.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20038 — Inferno Duck** (*Lived*): small inferno duck.
  - **20116 — Aradia's Perch** (*Lived*): Aradia dragonfly and poisoned sting.

#### 15.2 — The hungry child

A Verdani child forms mud cakes in the roots. Brunti asks what she needs and shares suitable food without promising to solve the entire region's suffering.

**Turn:** This action matters more to Sira than an elaborate magical theory.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20057 — Verdani Child** (*Lived*): hungry child forms mud cakes.

#### 15.3 — The grotto and its hunger

The Flytrap Grotto's buzzing presence raises a concrete hazard for locals. The company marks its approach for future travelers rather than staging an obligatory monster kill.

**Turn:** The group's care has an outward practical effect.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20037 — Flytrap Grotto** (*Lived*): flytrap grotto's terrible buzzing.

#### 15.4 — An unreliable mirror

At the Quicksilver Quagmire Brunti sees unfamiliar stars under silver water. She does not declare herself chosen or read a fixed future in their arrangement.

**Turn:** She leaves the pool without using a child as an excuse to obtain an artifact.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20077 — Quicksilver Quagmire** (*Lived*): silver quagmire's uncertain reflection.

#### 15.5 — The song in the fens

Sira relates another traveller's hearing of notes across misty Alluring Fens; this is a **reported** episode rather than a forced claim that the Vale and Karrhu's swamp are one locality.

**Turn:** The chapter ends on different sounds that need not come from one source.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20082 — Alluring Fens** (*Testimony*): night song across fens.

**Chapter handoff:** Sira remains with the group through Karrhu; her later work for the displaced communities will send her along a different ordinary route.

## Chapter 16 — Witnesses of Corruption

**Opening:** Velen spreads a collection of dated and undated accounts gathered in Aetherion and along the road, each attributed imperfectly.

**Present / source voices:** Brunti; Velen; Sira before their separation; recorded witnesses.

#### 16.1 — Ash that changes shape

The Witch's poisonous drift, Seer's hidden inner fire and Warlock's moving sigils are kept as three separate **archival descriptions**, each tied to its own scene of destruction.

**Turn:** Brunti wants a shared cause; Velen marks the reports' distinct origins.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20019 — Ashen Revenant Witch** (*Archive*): Ashen Witch and poison vapor.
  - **20020 — Ashen Revenant Seer** (*Archive*): Ashen Seer with inner fire.
  - **20030 — Ashen Revenant Warlock** (*Archive*): Warlock raises smoke symbols.

#### 16.2 — Rot and hunger

The Huntress's dead trail, Rootrot's swollen body and the Occultist's sapling-draining rite form a brief, uneasy documentary sequence, not a single battle in one forest.

**Turn:** Sira reminds the others that not every altered being chose its state.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20021 — Blightwraith Huntress** (*Archive*): Blightwraith Huntress kills growth.
  - **20024 — Blightwraith Rootrot** (*Archive*): Rootrot spreads decay.
  - **20029 — Blightwraith Occultist** (*Archive*): Occultist withers sapling.

#### 16.3 — Broken stone

Records describe a Crumblegheist guarding a cavern and two differently built beings breaking rocky ground. Give their movement and pressures individual sound signatures.

**Turn:** Velen refuses to identify a single creator from repeated cracks in stone.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20022 — Crumblegheist Mage** (*Archive*): Crumblegheist Mage guards cavern.
  - **20025 — Crumblegheist Roughian** (*Archive*): Roughian breaks rocky ground.
  - **20026 — Crumblegheist Barbarian** (*Archive*): Barbarian's armor and fissures.

#### 16.4 — The storm reports

Wildheart's certainty, Stormcaller's localized weather and Seductress's deceptive fragrance remain distinct witnesses' descriptions.

**Turn:** The party can recognize recurring danger without inventing a common command.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20023 — Tempestwailer Wildheart** (*Archive*): Wildheart coils lightning.
  - **20027 — Tempestwailer Stormcaller** (*Archive*): Stormcaller below canopy.
  - **20028 — Tempestwailer Seductress** (*Archive*): Seductress offers deceptive beauty.

#### 16.5 — The separation

Sira receives a legitimate message task for the displaced and takes a long overland route to seek contacts; Brunti and Velen continue north toward the Frostweaver records. The separation requires elapsed travel time, not instantaneous messenger magic.

**Turn:** Brunti now has fewer people to interrupt her certainty, and feels the absence.

**Source encounters:** Original connecting fiction; no direct encounter ID.

**Chapter handoff:** No universal corruption answer appears in the folios. The company now moves toward Everwinter to learn the limits of damaged historical records.

## Chapter 17 — The Mountain Has a Voice

**Opening:** The approach to the high country includes varying landscape and a long climb; the chapter does not assert unverified proximity between Sarga and Frostwind.

**Present / source voices:** Brunti; Velen; guides; the mountain's Grimnir merchant.

#### 17.1 — An earlier voice of the cliffs

On a separate earlier leg—given enough unmeasured travel time—the wind plays through the Cliffs of Sarga while a lioness watches a ledge. **Exact cartographic placement remains a review issue**; don't assert Sarga adjoins Frostwind.

**Turn:** A familiar stone sound fails to explain its own origin.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20099 — Cliffs of Sarga** (*Lived*): singing Cliffs of Sarga.
  - **20106 — Sarga Lioness** (*Lived*): lioness on cliff shelf.

#### 17.2 — The Frostwind climb

The mountain pass, snow-hidden recesses and an oddly scented balanced boulder become three different hazards or mysteries on the road.

**Turn:** Brunti chooses shelter when a route is unsafe rather than proving herself by haste.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20100 — Mountain Pass** (*Lived*): snowy narrow mountain pass.
  - **20102 — Frostwind Slopes** (*Lived*): Frostwind recess beneath snow.
  - **20103 — Strange Boulder** (*Lived*): perfume under precarious boulder.

#### 17.3 — Rock that resembles motion

The Giant's sheer face and Frost Strider's poised shape flank a tundra whose tiny hidden creatures matter. Neither rock formation is automatically animated.

**Turn:** Velen finds the living world more informative than the appearance of a step.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20105 — The Giant** (*Lived*): immense stone Giant.
  - **20121 — Frost Strider** (*Lived*): stone Frost Strider.
  - **20122 — Rocky Tundra** (*Lived*): life beneath rocky tundra.

#### 17.4 — Wolves and bronze

Embersnout crosses the snow; a bronze vault bears frost and a closed lock. Danger and temptation coexist without forcing a fight or an opening.

**Turn:** They leave the vault unclaimed and conserve warmth and supplies.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20109 — Embersnout** (*Lived*): Embersnout tracks across ice.
  - **20120 — Bronze Vault** (*Lived*): closed bronze vault.

#### 17.5 — The junk dealer

A Grimnir dealer has sorted oddments into misleading groups; a piece of old craft metal might resemble the memorial's casing but carries no proven provenance.

**Turn:** Brunti becomes better at declining attractive but weak evidence.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20061 — Frostwind Junkyard** (*Lived*): Frostwind junkdealer's oddments.

**Chapter handoff:** Their route reaches the Everwinter southern approaches slowly; hospitality will soon become necessary, not merely picturesque.

## Chapter 18 — The Last Warm Bowl

**Opening:** Snow and falling stone interrupt the northbound route before warmth returns at a trading post.

**Present / source voices:** Brunti; Velen; the Rokur postkeeper; Frostbite Fergus.

#### 18.1 — The broken way

A rockslide forces a turn; the postkeeper's account of a great Frostfang Alpha explains why another apparent shortcut is worse.

**Turn:** Brunti no longer measures bravery by the shortest distance.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20104 — Sudden Rockslide** (*Lived*): rockslide cuts off track.
  - **20124 — Frostfang Alpha** (*Testimony*): Frostfang Alpha described by locals.

#### 18.2 — The trader's fire

A Rokur woman serves stew and makes room for travelers. Describe food and mineral needs of the two visitors appropriately without treating Rokur flame as ordinary human temperature.

**Turn:** The party can ask where local accounts of older ruins are kept.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20123 — Everwinter Trading Post** (*Lived*): postkeeper offers stew.

#### 18.3 — A hungry fellow

Frostbite Fergus laughs, shifts on the log, and makes space. Give him appetite and humor without turning a friendly ice troll into a hidden quest dispenser.

**Turn:** Brunti enjoys company outside the framework of reward.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20132 — Frostbite Fergus** (*Lived*): Frostbite Fergus makes room.

#### 18.4 — The enormous bear

A Frostclaw Behemoth stands above an icebound path. Velen and Brunti avoid provoking it and wait for a safer passage.

**Turn:** The frozen world remains alive beyond the ruins.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20176 — Frostclaw Behemoth** (*Lived*): Frostclaw Behemoth rises above ice.

**Chapter handoff:** The keeper describes the Everwinter Guardian and Cave as a linked approach, not as a universal way to the Nekalos mechanism.

## Chapter 19 — Ice That Remembers

**Opening:** The valley preserves forms well enough to mislead anyone searching for a complete account.

**Present / source voices:** Brunti; Velen; guides or warnings provided by the postkeeper.

#### 19.1 — A chest that refuses warmth

Everwinter's carved chest holds a pocket of warmth that does not melt its ice. It is a singular local oddity, not Brunti's answer.

**Turn:** The distinction between an object and its interpretation is reinforced.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20129 — Everwinter Treasurechest** (*Lived*): chest warm but ice stays frozen.

#### 19.2 — Before the cave

The small ice-troll Guardian observes the approach; only after its scene does the Cave show preserved ancient shapes and pale runes. Passage may be negotiated or merely allowed without inventing a universally correct ritual.

**Turn:** Brunti refuses to call the frozen forms a complete history.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20130 — Everwinter Guardian** (*Lived*): Everwinter Guardian at cave.
  - **20131 — Everwinter Cave** (*Lived*): creatures sealed in ice.

#### 19.3 — People in the snow

Travelers struggle to orient in a blizzard; a report about Frostlace warns of an alluring voice leading people away. Keep the living lost travelers and the nymph's method distinct.

**Turn:** The party helps mark a safe line rather than following every whisper.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20136 — Lost Souls of Everwinter** (*Lived*): travelers lost in snowfall.
  - **20135 — Frostlace** (*Testimony*): Frostlace's deadly invitation.

#### 19.4 — What howls and guards

A guide recounts Misthowl's spectral warning, while a Winterwisp Warden is seen holding a narrow way. Neither is decoded into an oracle.

**Turn:** The company retreats before danger turns their search into exploitation.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20140 — Misthowl** (*Testimony*): Misthowl's uncertain warnings.
  - **20141 — Winterwisp Warden** (*Lived*): Winterwisp Warden blocks way.

**Chapter handoff:** The incomplete frozen inscriptions send Velen to seek Jorvald rather than asserting a reconstruction.

## Chapter 20 — The Broken Frostweaver Record

**Opening:** Everwinter's scholarly ruins make the difference between evidence and longing impossible to ignore.

**Present / source voices:** Brunti; Velen; Jorvald; fragmentary Frostweaver presences.

#### 20.1 — The scattered voices

Kaldric's broken cries, Lyria's almost vanished note and the Shattered Seer's separated fragments appear without an invented verified familial relation or origin for their condition.

**Turn:** Velen abandons the wish to read an unbroken narrative in damaged voices.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20133 — Kaldric of Nekalos** (*Lived*): Kaldric's fragmented cries.
  - **20139 — Lyria of Nekalos** (*Lived*): Lyria's faint lasting melody.
  - **20137 — The Shattered Seer** (*Lived*): Seer of Snows shattered voices.

#### 20.2 — Cold inscriptions

Nekalos's knowledge seal, a glint behind Frostfall Cascade and the Frostforged Vault offer different forms of guarded information; do not make all three one unlockable mechanism.

**Turn:** The group can learn where a question remains, not what must be behind each door.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20134 — Seal of Nekalos** (*Lived*): glowing Knowledge Seal.
  - **20142 — Frostfall Cascade** (*Lived*): glint behind frozen cascade.
  - **20143 — Frostforged Vault** (*Lived*): sealed Frostforged Vault.

#### 20.3 — Skyreach and the stag

A snow-cleared rise reveals Skyreach; a Spirit Stag carries an azure arrow. Brunti chooses aid for a living being over risking a summit for treasure.

**Turn:** Her changed priorities are now observable, not merely claimed.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20138 — Skyreach Peak** (*Lived*): Skyreach glimpsed in snow.
  - **20145 — Spirit Stag** (*Lived*): Spirit Stag wounded by azure arrow.

#### 20.4 — The scholar's refusal

Jorvald challenges the visitors among scattered scrolls. His limits about the Frostweaver past and Brunti's Grimnir memorial are explicit: he cannot give what he does not know.

**Turn:** Brunti has enough information to accept Darrik's safe transfer; a return is viable and due.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **20144 — The Scholar** (*Lived*): Jorvald's scattered books.

**Chapter handoff:** An ordinary delayed message from Sira reaches the travelers only after enough time to cover the road. A missing caravan child may have been sighted **near the Keldor border**, redirecting the journey for a living person rather than a hidden cosmic clue.

**Writing gate:** The geographically dispersed material is purposefully mediated by named travel intervals or testimony. Verify the final actual route against the canonical map and scene descriptions, and do not invent precise distances or travel duration.
