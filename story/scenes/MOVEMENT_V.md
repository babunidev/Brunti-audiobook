## Chapter 28 — A Story About the End

**Opening:** On the journey back, the living discuss Atranor without claiming to carry an eyewitness map of the gods' private realms.

**Present / perceived voices:** Brunti; a traveling mourner; Velen and Sira on an ordinary returning route as travel permits.

#### 28.1 — A story from Atranor

A traveler recounts **a mortal story about Atranor**—the polished dark plain, distant dragon forms and assembly beyond—as inherited tradition or disputed testimony. The place is canonically real; the speaker has no proved first-hand memory or license to pronounce a divine judgment.

**Turn:** Brunti now knows the difference between a meaningful story and an attested experience.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **1 — Atranor** (*Testimony*): uncertain account of Atranor judgment.

#### 28.2 — Returning without a cure

A message from Darrik says the affected panel remains safe in isolation and the convergence work is approaching. Brunti chooses prompt return rather than another expedition.

**Turn:** The last obstacle is not defeating a monster but admitting uncertainty before people she respects.

**Source encounters:** Original linking fiction; no direct encounter source ID.

**Chapter handoff:** The homeward passage allows real rest and time; no teleportation, recovered death-memory or prophecy is added.

## Chapter 29 — The Work of Bringing Back

**Opening:** The Skyland has continued to move while Brunti was gone; Darrik has kept its chambers safe without waiting for a heroic solution.

**Present / perceived voices:** Brunti; Darrik; craft witnesses; Sira and Velen only if ordinary travel schedules allow.

#### 29.1 — The public account

Brunti names her harmful brace, the incomplete Aetherion drawing and the dangerous Blackwood decision. She presents the latter as uncertain experience rather than evidence and does not pretend it solved the memorial.

**Turn:** Darrik can evaluate her account without agreeing to her old plan.

**Source encounters:** Original linking fiction; no direct encounter source ID.

#### 29.2 — The two marks

Side-by-side inspection of the protected panel, the incomplete archive sketch and surviving toolmarks identifies two component memorials, Tavra and Korrum. Evidence must be replicable by other craft workers; do not invent a new magical resonance law.

**Turn:** The problem has a local historical explanation, and both people's identities can remain distinct.

**Source encounters:** Original linking fiction; no direct encounter source ID.

#### 29.3 — The inhabited wall

Darrik installs safe structure in the resting area. Brunti assists in moving the conserved memorials to an accessible listening place, maintaining separation of echoes and visible labels. The residents' repair-rest remains safe.

**Turn:** The community's practical responsibilities and ancestral care are reconciled through work, not decree.

**Source encounters:** Original linking fiction; no direct encounter source ID.

**Chapter handoff:** An ordinary task remains after the celebration. Brunti chooses to do it before asking what anyone thinks of her courage.

## Chapter 30 — The Note That Remains

**Opening:** The new archive has two audible rhythms and a pause between them; the pause is not a defect to be repaired.

**Present / perceived voices:** Brunti; Darrik; a young Grimnir listener; residents.

#### 30.1 — A change that cannot be counted

The game label **Level up!** becomes only a literary counterpart: Brunti now checks the needs of the listener and the condition of the space before acting. No number, reward screen, advancement announcement or stat is narrated.

**Turn:** Her growth is evident without anyone proclaiming it.

**Source encounters:** 
  - **2 — Level up!** (*Metaphor*): earned change without literal level-up.

#### 30.2 — The unfilled space

When a child asks what happened inside Blackwood, Brunti separates observation from supposition and suggests listening to other accounts too. A familiar tool rests unused; the Skyland hum and an ordinary meal complete the scene.

**Turn:** The world continues moving, neither solved nor abandoned.

**Source encounters:** Original linking fiction; no direct encounter source ID.

**Chapter handoff:** The book stops on a lived gesture, not a final answer to Morkahja.

**Writing gate:** Verify original encounter description, character identity and regional geography at the moment of prose drafting. Preserve unproven aspects of Keldor, Blackwood, the Ravage and Atranor; never let Brunti's unreliable memory silently become world canon.
