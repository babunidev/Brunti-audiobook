## Interpretation key

*Expansion* is a game-content association, not necessarily an in-world travel location. Base/unspecified encounters (expansion 0) must not all be located in one region. *Function* translates the source game encounter type into a **preliminary literary category**. *Subtype* and *element* are game metadata and can be empty. *Follow* means the game explicitly names its next encounter. *Placement* means the exact place and narrative form in the future manuscript; every record currently remains **Unplaced**.

The book may present an entry as a lived scene, testimony, story, archive, song, memory, dream or recurring motif. The final adaptation must make each source encounter findable by numeric ID; adding a row to this table does not constitute integration.
