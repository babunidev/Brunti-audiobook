## Recurring pattern to inspect in Stage 3

**Qualitative observation, not a machine-counted defect:** many chapters culminate in an unusual sign or creature → speculative relation to Brunti's wall → refusal to overclaim → notation of a blank → onward journey. The following blocks need sustained read-aloud comparison:

- **Ch3–9 (Vale/Eldertree):** nature, trade, boundaries and objects offer opportunities for varied intimacy, playfulness and uneventful wonder.
- **Ch17–20 (Frostwind/Everwinter):** physical survival and shared hospitality must feel different from archival proof and philosophical caution.
- **Ch21–24 (Keldor search):** locate missing child as a concrete interpersonal goal; do not let twenty Chapter 24 encounters bury Brunti's knowingly costly personal choice.
- **Ch25–28 (Blackwood/afterlife):** keep cumulative strangeness, human recovery and inherited Atranor story distinct in texture and scene purpose.

**Scene-function tags for later granular audit:** `hazard`, `relief`, `relationship`, `humor`, `testimony`, `discovery`, `mistake`, `cost`, `respite`, `mythic encounter`. Tags must describe actual rendered prose, not merely a game encounter type. An encounter ID's mere presence in the matrix does not prove the scene has earned its place.

## Stage 0 conclusions and limits

The narrative **does** have a coherent macro-arc: unsafe local repair → documentary inquiry → increasingly consequential service → accepted safe return option → voluntary Blackwood mistake → injury and accountability → locally justified memorial repair. Its instability lies in the precise clock, how the late mistake grows from Brunti's earlier choices, and repeated thematic closure.
