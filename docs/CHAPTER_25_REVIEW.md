## Source tension: Guardian and portal conditions

The **game encounter for 19667** states *“Only a forgotten password or an item of particular significance can grant passage.”* That is a **specific encounter proposition**, not a proved rule of the world. The canonical [Blackwood Abyss account](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Blackwood_Abyss.md) reports witnesses naming passwords, offerings, objects, ancestral memory or recognition, but **none agree and no reliable condition has been established**. It likewise reports **no confirmed entry mechanism** for the high cliff's Blackwood Portal; many find it and still cannot open it. [Guardians and Spirits](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Creatures/Guardians_and_Spirits.md) repeats the lack of a reliable passage condition.

The novel **retains both accounts and their tension openly**: Brunti remembers the game account's proposed condition, consciously refuses to counterfeit a password with the seven letters or call a significant tool a key, and subsequently lacks reliable memory of passing the threshold. Her arrival on the far side **does not prove** the Guardian yielded or approved an object. The ambiguity is intentional and remains canonically unclassified. No previous chapter's artifact provides a hidden magic gate formula.

## Cross-chapter causal continuity

[Chapter 24](../manuscript/chapters/24-paths-among-the-dead.md) ended with **Brunti alone beneath a dark cliff**, unable to locate the return route and hearing a sound above it, with **no door yet observed**. Chapter 25 begins with that whisper and reveals the wooden door above. **Sira and Velen (they/them) do not enter Blackwood** and remain with the practical relief and archives outside Keldor's deeper region. Their concerns are represented through Brunti's fallible memory, not as actual visiting characters or new evidence. Brunti is an embodied **Grimnir of stone, metal and crystal**, experiencing mineral/joint fatigue and a small wrist fracture, not human blood, sweating or unbounded subterranean endurance. [Grimnir Life and Care](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Traditions/Grimnir_Life_and_Care.md) supports water/mineral sustenance, rest and the possibility of worsening deep isolation without requiring immediate Stonebound transformation.

The scene distinguishes **last reliable observation**, **possibly distorted perception**, **explicit missing memory**, and **novel-only material choices**. Brunti records observed roots and discontinuities but never establishes a connection to Tavra, Korrum, Kaldric/Nekalos, the earlier seven engraved letters or any cause of Keldor's curse. The practical reason for entering is her **unwise voluntary relapse** after the search and rescue ended, not an authorized mission, a newly missing companion or a destiny imposed by Blackwood. Her material engineering improves a torn pack strap; it does not solve the abyss.

The Guardian scene ends with Brunti unable to account for the transition to its far side. Near-inverted silhouettes and a sudden swallowing of her footsteps begin the next movement: **Chapter 26 must let the Hollow Choir's hanging faces and chorus become fully present rather than assuming they were already experienced**. Chapter 27 must still preserve the end of the following source chain, especially the healer's ordinary explanation of survival without new cosmic certainty.
