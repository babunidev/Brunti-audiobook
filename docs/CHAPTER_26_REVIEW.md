## Literary progression and limitations

Chapter 25 ends immediately after the Blackwood Guardian passage gap at a cavern's entrance, where inverted trees and a strange muffling of footsteps first become perceivable. Chapter 26 begins there without contradicting missing portal/Guardian passage conditions. The Choir introduces sound as unreliable supposed memory; the Mire converts this to unreliable images; the Procession presents a recognizable body moving independently in an uninterrupted physical line. The three dangers form a tightening thematic argument against Brunti's wish to make every likeness a clue. At the arch Brunti has **not** found reliable evidence, a named memory, an exit or the metaphysical cause of Keldor. Her own notebook records only distinct observations and uncertainties; personal shame and longing do not become external facts.
