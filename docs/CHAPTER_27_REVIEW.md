## Novel continuity and causal turn

The opening picks up precisely where [Chapter 26](../manuscript/chapters/26-the-voices-beneath.md) ends: Brunti follows the silent, hooded procession **toward** the dark arch of a colossal structure, having **never identified the Brunti-like marcher**. In Chapter 27 the marchers are no longer visible after the threshold, so their origin/destination within the architecture remains unclear. The first direct staging of the **Silent Throne interior** is now this chapter, not an earlier scene.

Brunti's motive is a voluntary **relapse**: Chapter 24 established that the rescued Verdani child was already safe and the road home open, with Sira and Velen refusing to join her. Chapters 25–26 separated witnessed sensory phenomena from identification; Chapter 27 brings that question to its peak. The enormous living-looking cathedral reflects Blackwood lore, but **is not a proved explanation of the Urcogor Ravage, Keldor's history, the double Grimnir memorial, Nekalos or the seven carved letters**. The local craft solution remains anchored to an Aetherion sketch, original memorial toolmarks and Darrik's safe structural plan, to be tested on her ordinary return.

A severe bodily breakdown follows the hall's pulses, leading to an **explicit blank**, with no witnessed portal or exit. She wakes in the **canonical Keldor-edge Verdani lazaret**. Recovery uses culturally and physiologically sensible [Grimnir life and care](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Traditions/Grimnir_Life_and_Care.md): mineral-rich water and nourishment, supported resting positions, stabilization of crystal joints and restrained movement; **not bleeding, sweating, instantaneous regeneration or inappropriate human biological mechanisms**. The [Verdani race](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Races/Verdani.md) supports coherent living-water bodies with antlers and hydromancy. The healer's name and the exact rescuers' route are **not established by game source** and deliberately not fabricated.

Only **after several days and ordinary road travel** do **Sira** (who continues her real relief work outside the abyss) and **Velen (they/them)** (carrying documents from stations) reach the lazaret. This preserves their refusal to enter Blackwood. Sira does **not praise Brunti's unnecessary venture**, openly names fear and anger, and accepts neither risk nor apology as a magical undoing. Velen provides a **clean witness notebook**, recording last confirmed impressions, unremembered gaps and unverified interpretation separately. Brunti states in writing that she brought back **no object, no working method and no verified connection to the memorial**. The final original fictional scene has Brunti **agree to go home** while still needing treatment and ordinary travel. There is no teleportation, instantaneous wellness, finished reunion with the rescued child, final answer to the world, or assumed Darrik letter delivery.

**Next Chapter 28 handoff:** ordinary convalescent return journey from the Keldor border, an uncertain **mortal testimony about Atranor** rather than Brunti as eyewitness to a divine domain, and the next message/decision about Darrik's safely isolated memorial.
