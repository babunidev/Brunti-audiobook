## Source encounter 1 — Atranor, and its mode

The full game entry is indexed as JSON key **`00001`**, numeric **`mEncounterID=1`**, and title **Atranor**. It was reread in its entirety directly from [Morkahja at `f62708ce2dd4`](https://github.com/babunidev/Morkahja/blob/f62708ce2dd455f0fd77b183a87c705888bc4a59/server/game/encountersdata.json), data blob `8d5f254a5aa9078cc91e3c0d8d4e6c4022f6712f`; `mEncounterFollow=0`. The entry text **has not changed** since the previous baseline. The 203-record equality check identified only seven other **`mEncounterText`-only** revisions (20138–20144).

The *game scene itself* presents Atranor as an arrival of a soul rather than walking into a mortal location. It describes a polished endless **black stone plain** with broken reflections, silver sky without sun or moon, moving constellations in several colors, **luminous rivers/currents**, enormous pillars fading into mist, **inverted forests, upward waterfalls and floating elemental landscapes**, enormous quiescent divine dragons merging with ruins and stairs, the realm revealing **all a life's kindnesses, cruelties, decisions and regrets**, and a spontaneously appearing **path to a circular platform and the watching Pantheon**. No simple spoken verdict or achieved judgment is described; the possibility remains of a changed direction rather than an ultimate ending.

**Adaptation mode: `Testimony` (one of the 203 unique IDs, first-drafted in Chapter 28).** The mortal mourner Tarnel explicitly says he is **retelling an inherited story told by his late sister and her grandmother**, not personally observing Atranor, conducting a séance or being resurrected. His account includes the major perceptible features and the life-revealing path; Velen and Sira ask for distinctions. Brunti **never dies, travels to Atranor, communes with a judge or receives a divine sign**. The narrated image remains within the reported story, separate from the novel's actual roadside shelter and homeward journey. The name and private bereavement of Tarnel, his family's keep-sake case, and the conversation's particular words are **original novel fiction**.

## Required world laws checked against exact canonical texts

[Pinned MorkahjaWorld Atranor](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Atranor.md), [Divine Laws](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Pantheon/Divine_Laws.md), and the [Elemental Pantheon](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Pantheon/Elemental_Pantheon.md) were reopened. They establish the **unconditional passage of a soul to Atranor when its body's last life-sustaining fragment of divine essence returns to its god**, and its retention of conscious identity; the judgment's **three specifically responsible deities are Aerisara, Sylvanus, and the deity whose essence was held last**. Resurrection back into the former life and its preserved memories requires **at least one god voluntarily gifting new essence**, not all twelve and not a guaranteed favorable roll or four sacred items. If not resurrected, a soul enters the **last-essence deity's inner realm** with identity and choice intact. All souls have an **unconditional right to reincarnation** at their own chosen time, choosing a new form with gifts from several gods; no deity may hold them against their will. Reincarnation **does not transmit conscious memory of past mortal or divine lives**, although unexplained affinities may remain. The physical beginnings of a reincarnated life and inner experience of divine realms remain unknown. The gods **cannot foresee a predetermined future**: Morkahja's future is unwritten.

Tarnel and the others recount/reason about the material in ordinary language without inventing a new heavenly witness, tribunal, death test, resurrection guarantee, destined reunion or proof that a particular child is a former relative. His sister's speculative comfort is **not** authoritative testimony about what she experienced after death. Although the game Atranor entry speaks as if the soul directly perceives these wonders, the novel deliberately keeps that staging **inside an attributed inherited telling**, preserving the matrix's planned mode.

The room of the Blackwood Abyss, composite Grimnir memorial, Shattered Seer and Atranor are **not physically connected by shared motifs**. Tavra and Korrum's stones contain **fixed mortal traces**, not enslaved conscious souls or fresh posthumous messages. Brunti's resemblance-driven longing is treated with sympathy but does not override the world's reincarnation rule or create new metaphysical rules.

## Continuity, character placement, grounded journey

Chapter 27 ends at the **Keldor-edge Verdani lazaret** with Brunti choosing to return home despite a still-braced **fractured left crystal wrist**; Sira and Velen had reached the lazaret only by ordinary road travel **after** she awoke. Chapter 28 opens after sufficient treatment to permit **short stages of wagon travel, staffed accommodation, mineral nourishment, rest and fatigue**, not a miraculous recovery or instant teleportation. All three begin on the safe route away from Keldor. Sira accompanies them partway and then **continues her own obligation delivering relief messages** via the eastward staffed route. **Velen (they/them)** remains with Brunti to carry the sourced Aetherion sketch and separate notebook. The recovered Verdani girl's actual family reunion is explicitly **still unverified**, not retroactively asserted as complete; the lost boy remains within care per earlier station report.

Brunti's **Skyland** remains an *engineered drifting city of stone/metal and geomantic anchors*, not an ordinary naturally floating island; canonical [Grimnir Skylands](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Grimnir_Skylands.md) supports known moorings, drifting routes and approaching convergence, but **no exact physical itinerary or calendar is asserted**. Stages include ordinary bridges, mill, roadside houses, shelter, riverside station, highland lift and an approaching Skyland; the highland sighting is **not falsely identified as Brunti's own**. Ch28 closes **before Brunti is back inside her own home**; Chapter 29 still owns arrival, public explanation, physical inspection, and repairing/restoring the memorial in a protected location.

**Darrik letter chronology:** earlier chapters established that Brunti had **sent** relevant sketch/news to Darrik but had **not verified receipt**. The new letter first supplies proof of receipt, confirms that Darrik kept the unsafe panel **isolated and the inhabited resting chamber safe**, and that a **replacement and safe transfer** are being prepared as the convergence nears. It does **not** claim an already completed transfer or definitive identification of Tavra and Korrum. Brunti's eventual testimony must separate the verifiable Aetherion diagram and physical toolmarks from unverified Blackwood impressions. The close is a small **asked-for repair of a loading frame**, not the literal `Level up!` encounter reserved for Chapter 30.

## Chapter 29 continuity correction — 18 November 2026

Chapter 28's original mourner was initially drafted with the personal name **Orven**, unintentionally duplicating **Orven, the established Skykeeper introduced in Chapter 2** who provides independently attributed field accounts and later returns to the home council. The Chapter 28 mourner has been consistently renamed **Tarnel** throughout Chapter 28 and its review. This is a novel-only continuity repair, not a game/world lore revision; the mourner's Atranor inherited testimony, original provenance and chapter length (**5,780 words**) are unchanged.
