## Exact follow sequence and plot continuity

The source of **20188** explicitly records `mEncounterFollow=19444`. The source **19444** has `mEncounterFollow=0`. Chapter 10 follows **seal → entered sanctuary**, with no shortcut or prior sight of the inner chamber as a separate visit. World lore states the seal requires *understanding of the magic holding it*, but gives **no approved universal lock code**. The gentle local-fabric-and-fitting mechanism is a first-draft plot invention. Its future consistency and security implications require authorial review; it has not been added to the game or world lore.

The travelers begin Chapter 10 following Chapter 9's road out of Eldertree toward Aetherion, encounter Aru in the Vale, then travel via **eastern meadows and a northern sanctuary spur over additional road days**. These are ordinary roads with a deliberately **unverified** map, not an instantaneous teleportation or a claim of exact distances. The extra nights cost real time against the Skyland convergence, preserving the personal stakes. Brunti continues toward Aetherion with Thalen's catalog contact and the **original unsolved transport ledger notation**, entering no archive and finding no composite-memorial answer in this chapter.

Novel-only supporting people: **Aru** (Rokur pilgrim), **Tessan** (Aevis courier), the unnamed roadside merchants/bandits and reed-seller. **Sira** keeps her own responsibilities as a message carrier and refuses to let Brunti choose help on another person's behalf.
