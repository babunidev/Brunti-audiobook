- **Sira is central:** She sees the home and instruments of a deceased friend mentioned in Chapter 12, hears that her relative/acquaintance **Alen** is helping, and remains with people she knows. Her grief does not become a collectible witness account. The names **Alen, Renn, Tavi** and the friend’s unspoken personal identity are inventions for the novel, not additions to game canon.
- **Brunti's task is materially bounded:** The village's waterwork is not magically repaired all at once. **Three feeders** operate; the fourth is marked, intentionally closed and still awaits a correctly sized pipe. Brunti and Velen travel **briefly to a nearby supply station** to procure it while **Sira stays with Alen**. This group separation must resolve in Chapter 14 **before** any depicted three-person passage into Ohgar.

A **different, separately mentioned young traveler with a carved walking stick** traveled with a **previous group**, and the caravan later **divided at a station**. The child's actual whereabouts and whether they are missing remain **unconfirmed**; **no evidence places the child in Keldor**. This preserves the story spine's planned later warning without asserting it prematurely. The child in Chapter 9's reported river-community message is not silently declared identical.

Distant woodland and haunted-forest testimony do **not** create nearby extra locations. The exact Evergrove–Ohgar distances and multi-day travel chronology remain unverified.
