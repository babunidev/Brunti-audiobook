## Risk register: concrete actionable issues, not invented contradictions

### P1 — Ohgar → Karrhu: strengthen the regional corridor, not the itinerary

**Evidence:** [Chapter 14](../manuscript/chapters/14-words-in-the-sand.md) exits the desert by marked, guided caravan roads; rain gathers and a longer route around bridge trouble is chosen. [Chapter 15](../manuscript/chapters/15-beneath-the-reeds.md) opens at the Karrhu ferry. Map places **Ohgar south of central Vale** and **Karrhu east**, not directly adjacent. This is not impossible, but the broad east/northeast transfer is *underdescribed*. **Suggested minimal prose bridge (proposal, not inserted):** identify that the caravan returns to a settled cross-regional road before turning **east toward the low wetlands**, with intervening days/stations if supported by the chosen novel clock. Do not claim an official named road or precise distance from the screenshot.

### P2 — Karrhu → Sarga / Frostwind: name the long approach and keep Sarga unplaced

**Evidence:** [Chapter 16](../manuscript/chapters/16-witnesses-of-corruption.md) has a three-day shared journey and then Sira's westbound/Brunti's northbound caravan split. [Chapter 17](../manuscript/chapters/17-the-mountain-has-a-voice.md) says the northern journey takes longer than maps suggest, with two caravan changes and multiple days of climbing; **Sarga marked in the margin of a trader map**, before Frostwind. Map gives Karrhu east of Vale, Frostwind north of it, but does **not** place Sarga. [Frostwind Landmarks](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Frostwind_Landmarks.md) expressly states the cliffs' precise position relative to the range is unresolved. **Suggested minimal bridge (proposal):** add a named relative *direction* or road pattern from Karrhu's northern crossings toward the Vale's mountain approaches before the Sarga account; **do not fabricate a plotted Sarga coordinate or equate the cliffs with a mapped Frostwind site**.

### P3 — Ch28 “northbound” while returning to Skylands: clarify destination, not absolute compass

**Evidence:** [Chapter 28](../manuscript/chapters/28-a-story-about-the-end.md) says Tarnel joins a **“northbound caravan”** shared by Brunti, Sira and Velen. Later the narrative plainly states they reach **upland landing stations** used by the moving Skylands, with transfer only when the receiving Skyland confirms its approach. Map associates Grimnir Skylands mostly with Celesterra in the southwest, but [mobile Skylands](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Grimnir_Skylands.md) cannot be fixed to that single map symbol. **Suggested minimal change (proposal):** replace “northbound caravan” with **“caravan bound for the upland landing routes”** if the specific northward side-leg is unnecessary. Alternatively explicitly explain a northbound transfer followed by a change of direction. **Not a confirmed canonical mistake.**

- **The world map is schematic**, and the implied climatic progression from Vale/Ohgar desert to Karrhu swamp to Everwinter snow is enhanced by real interim caravan/road scenes. Some sources do not determine exact distances.
