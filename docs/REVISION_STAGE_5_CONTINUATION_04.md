**Most important source-authority tension (not a silent fix):**

- The game `20192 Nereval Elite` specifies **“Only those who bear the river's mark may enter.”** The novel does not establish Brunti, Sira or Velen as bearers of a literal marked token or personal rune.
- But the **established [MorkahjaWorld Nereval's Sanctuary](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Nerevals_Sanctuary.md)** explicitly says the seal **responds to an act of healing**, including damaged water source, sick plant, creature or person. The [Ethera's Vale lore](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Etheras_Vale.md) also calls healing a condition of pilgrim entry. The game `19001` shows the dark **mark of restoration** waiting for a healing gesture.
- The novel depicts sincere watercourse repair reaching the plant's roots, then final reinforcement of the channel, before the restoration seal illuminates. **That action matches world lore and the game linked encounter `19001`.** It diverges from the *literal-bearer phrasing* of `20192`. The guardian recasts the mark as demonstrated river understanding; **this is a draft literary reconciliation across two differing source statements**, not proof that the game's literal bearer rule is inapplicable.
- The game's requested sequence **20192 → 19001** is preserved as guardian first, seal/entry second. **Editorial sign-off remains open** on whether the game mark must also exist as literal personal identification. Do not rewrite either authority by inference.

**Geography:** The game's `20064` mentions ancient trees inside Evergrove while canonical [Evergrove](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Evergrove.md) is an oasis-bearing desert. Sira's account specifically locates the tree-covered cave within cultivated oasis growth, maintaining both observable features and established desert identity. The onward crossing from southeastern Vale into mapped oasis routes is gradual, not teleportation.
