## Plot evidence: what Chapter 11 does and does not resolve

An **invented, incomplete archive restoration sketch** shows **two intended stone positions beneath one shared covering**, with a damaged transport-sign fragment resembling—but not exactly matching—Brunti's ledger notation. Velen copies and measures only the **surviving** sheet, marks gaps and requires further provenance. **No original maker, stone names, dates, exact memorial identity, universal restoration method or proof of the Skyland's historical purpose is recovered**. The later planned names **Tavra/Korrum** are not prematurely revealed. Physical comparison and return to the actual wall remain necessary in Chapters 29–30.

Velen is introduced as **they/them** Aevis archivist. Their appointment to accompany Brunti is **requested but not granted** by the end of this draft; the subsequent chapter must settle the request before treating them as a travel companion. They distinguish their personal work and archive custody from Brunti's desire to remove the fragile original. Sira has separate spoken-message delivery obligations, including a novel-only garment for a child's instrument.

Aetherion's market, Archive, Ember Wards and Garden belong to the **same central Vale city**, which is governed by an **Elemental Assembly**, not a single ruler. World lore does not specify exact district-to-district travel times; the chapter moves between them by ordinary streets over **at least two days**. Aetherion is above Ethera's living heart; the chapter does **not** invent a newly verified hidden fifth current or a universal answer to all resonances.
