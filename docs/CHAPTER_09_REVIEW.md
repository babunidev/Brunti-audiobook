## Original narrative connecting choices

- **Pel** is the talkative cloth seller from the Chapter 8 wayhouse; his first-person/second-hand accounts are differentiated. The hidden market and Celesterra are **not treated as adjacent on the same physical walk**.
- **Orena** is an invented pilgrim receiving an attributed spoken river-family message. Sira retains independent duties and does not become Brunti's explanatory companion alone; private messages not addressed to Orena remain undisclosed.
- The travelers follow a road from Eldertree toward the **Pantheon Temple south of Eldridia**, then visit a **bookbinder near Eldertree's southern edge** while traveling toward Aetherion. This is a plausible novel detour, but exact road length and mapped location are not verified.
- **Thalen** makes no claim to know the memorial's maker. The ledger mark is still a **transport notation** of uncertain meaning; the Archive of Echoes is a reference for further catalog research. **Velen** in Chapter 11 must still identify the partial two-stone composite sketch through actual archival work, not a pre-solved note.
- The chapter's binding metaphor continues Brunti's active character arc without inventing a single world-wide cause of resonances or treating Pantheon Temple as a quest unlock.
