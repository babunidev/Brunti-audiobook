## Important plot continuity

**Chapter 13's split is actually resolved on page:** Brunti and Velen return from a local Evergrove supply station to the devastated village, **rejoin Sira** and help reopen its **fourth feeder** with Alen and Renn controlling the inspection and water allocation. Reopened water **does not equal** fully rebuilt houses; the socket needs longer-term work and continued monitoring. They leave the village together only after several days. The community continues independently.

**Overland travel:** An ordinary sequence of oasis stations, salt flats, wells and guided caravans leads **from desert Evergrove into Ohgar** without teleportation. Guide **Sael** and traveler **Nera** are original literary characters. After Ohgar, the party takes mapped routes toward the wetter approaches of **Karrhu**; the chapter explicitly does **not** place Ohgar and Karrhu side by side or assume a complete map. Exact distances, travel/season chronology and convergence timing remain to be verified.

**Personal evidence:** Brunti keeps separate the **incomplete Aetherion two-bed drawing** (novel-only first discovered in Chapter 11), the seven unparsed Vale letters and the unread Ohgar disc. She sends Darrik a **sealed, source-limited report** of the drawing via trade courier, requesting no unsafe postponement of the local memorial protection/transfer; **delivery is not confirmed**. She retains her copy. The local Skyland problem is not solved, and neither Ohgar's inscription nor the granite groan is connected to it by invented prophecy.
