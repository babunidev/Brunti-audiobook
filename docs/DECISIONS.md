# Creative direction and decisions

## 17 November 2026 — stated by Babuni

- Build a dedicated GitHub workspace for a fantasy adventure audiobook derived from **all** Morkahja encounters.
- Use a **young female Grimnir warrior** chosen from game avatar data.
- Use that character's source name as a working book title.
- Make encounters part of a larger narrative; they may be lived by Brunti or retold by others, and need not follow gameplay solutions.
- Work step by step, first establishing a strong documented foundation rather than drafting the book at once.

## Working selection (not permanent canon)

**Brunti**, female Grimnir warrior **portrait 104**, with Grimnir **frame 75**, is the working protagonist and title. Portrait 78 is a same-name rogue, not the selected warrior.

## Open creative proposals

- A memory-stone resonance anomaly as a possible inciting incident.
- Five broad narrative movements, not yet chapters.
- Questions of memory, belonging, protection and listening as potential thematic tensions.
- Relationships, chronology, antagonism, ending, audio treatment and final title.

An AI-generated proposal requires Babuni's creative approval; its presence in files is not an approval record. Any old game descriptions that conflict with the current Skylands lore remain flagged, not silently harmonized.

## 17 November 2026 — delegated working-fiction direction

In the subsequent conversation, Babuni explicitly told ChatGPT to make the next creative choices and proceed with the story. This delegates **literary drafting choices**, not amendments to the canonical MorkahjaWorld repository or a claim that each beat sheet has been independently reviewed. ChatGPT (**GPT-6**) selected the [current story spine](../story/STORY_SPINE.md), [cast and character arcs](../story/CHARACTER_ARCS.md) and [30-chapter causal chain](../story/CAUSAL_CHAIN.md).

Working fiction choices: Brunti causes a local resonance safety problem while bracing a composite memorial panel. Darrik is a conscientious Grimnir building steward, Sira a novel-named extension of the source Bloomwalker, and Velen an original Aevis archivist. The locally recoverable memorial identities are Tavra and Korrum, two original deceased Grimnir. The ending safely rehouses both memorials rather than forcing their old voices into a single rhythm. The Blackwood/Atranor/Ravage mysteries remain governed by established lore, and the plot cannot imply a new universal magical cause.

**Approval distinction:** Creative drafting is authorized and the working direction is selected; this is **not** an independent human line edit, canonical ratification, finished chapter or release approval. The user's original creative brief and world authorship remain credited to Babuni.
