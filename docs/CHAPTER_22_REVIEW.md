Ivera, a linen courier from the shelter, provides a corroborating ordinary destination and a separate warning; a stream crossing gives Brunti a practical repair-and-route-marking task without suggesting a universal magical solution. The narrative still does not claim that the child's location is proven, that the two altered Gobbls share a curse source or that bandits control all Keldor roads.
