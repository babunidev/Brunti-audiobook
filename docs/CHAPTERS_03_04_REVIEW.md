## Cross-chapter continuity and limitations

- **Chapter 2 → 3:** A real trade descent and Eda's shared caravan road replace any unstated portal or airborne teleportation. The precise landing site and distances are **not verified** against a cartographic source.
- **Chapter 3 → 4:** Eda escorts Brunti until the Arcadian Hills side track and then continues toward Aetherion. Brunti's later detours are hers; Sira joins **only at the end of chapter 4**, in the Ethereal Glades.
- **Source text vs location lore:** World Vale landmarks list **Tranquil Wilds** but leave seasonal changes undescribed; the **game encounter explicitly says autumn**, so the adaptation treats the scene as Eda's autumn memory during the current summertime journey.
- **Season and time:** Summer flowers/butterfly and a remembered autumn fox may coexist without claiming a single day's impossible season. Chapter 4 takes multiple days; the exact travel distance/time and council deadline remain **unchecked**.
- **Letter geography:** Canon places the named landscapes within the Vale but does not provide one proven seven-stone walking route. The novel invents a plausible path sequence without claiming to have mapped canonical distances.
- **Sira:** A young Verdani musician bearing messages fits the Bloomwalker source identity, but “Sira,” the partnership and their conversation are new audiobook fiction.
- **The two sounds:** Any similarity of Earthsinger's staff or the Enigmatic Prairies' notes to the memorial's two notes is narrative **analogy**, not evidence of shared origins. Brunti does not discover a global corruption cause, coded prophecy or new magic law.
- **Narration:** Main text remains close third-person past tense. Reported encounters have identified tellers and are not staged as events Brunti saw. No inline source tags or production directions appear in the spoken prose.
