## Continuity and literary direction

The chapter continues directly from [Chapter 19](../manuscript/chapters/19-ice-that-remembers.md) with **Brunti, Velen and guide Enna**. **Sira remains physically apart**, fulfilling her own caravan and relief obligations, and does not join the northern scenes. Named winter stations, many journeys and distinct branches between the shrine, palace, cascade, vault, peak and Jorvald **avoid portraying dispersed sites as adjacent**. Travel intervals are deliberately approximate and **not verified against a final canonical map**. The nine sources are observed in the order of the scene plan, with a route guide and ordinary weather constraints. Jorvald does not suddenly materialize near a treasure.

The ancient Frostweaver mysteries are left open. Source [Everwinter Figures](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/People/Everwinter_Figures.md) establishes the haunted Kaldric, intangible Lyria, Shattered Seer and guarded scholar Jorvald, including explicit uncertainty over Kaldric/Lyria connection and causes. [Frostweaver Palace Ruins](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Frostweaver_Palace_Ruins.md), [Frostfall Cascade](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Frostfall_Cascade.md), [Frostforged Vault](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Frostforged_Vault.md) and [Skyreach Peak](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Skyreach_Peak.md) confirm these as distinct sites with unresolved content. She sends a supportive letter through ordinary couriers without falsely asserting timely arrival or cancelling Darrik's independent authorized plan.
