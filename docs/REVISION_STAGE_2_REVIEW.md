## R6 — keep Brunti's particular flaw visible after her lesson

The original Chapter 20 already accepted the **safe transfer** and sought an honest document instead of a miraculous solution. Rather than retract that growth, revisions differentiate:

- [Ch1](../manuscript/chapters/01-skyland-morning.md): Brunti's trained judgment as a terrace guard fails her when the court *looks* empty; she forgets an unseen inhabited room can be endangered by a structural brace.
- [Ch11](../manuscript/chapters/11-the-city-of-listening.md): she can answer strangers' questions but worries about admitting to her **own council** that the two dead remain unidentified.
- [Ch20](../manuscript/chapters/20-the-broken-frostweaver-record.md): the actual outward report is accurate and practical, yet she **omits** her dread of standing beside the caretaker and explaining why her investigation cannot finish the ancestral record.
- [Ch24](../manuscript/chapters/24-paths-among-the-dead.md): that specifically personal dread, not a fresh discovery, drives her wrong decision. She seeks an impressive finding that might make her return feel worthy of those she endangered. She *knows* the analogy to Keldor does not justify a mission.
- [Ch29](../manuscript/chapters/29-the-work-of-bringing-back.md): she admits fear of an incomplete account helped her choose Blackwood, distinct from physical hurt or the loss of a possible clue.

**Character result:** Growth in restraint toward strangers is genuine. The late regression has a narrower blind spot in **homecoming, professional honor and accountability** rather than abruptly erasing months of learning. The prose does not frame the regression as a hidden correct way to learn the memorial identities.

## R8 — establish a mundane recovery edge, not an explanation for the abyss

[Ch27](../manuscript/chapters/27-what-the-throne-keeps.md) now includes the healer relaying an ordinary incident report from the outer-route wardens:

1. **Two outer-route wardens** checking a broken warning cord find Brunti **at first light beyond the last maintained path**, beside an old boundary stone and disused haul track. Neither saw her arrive.
2. They carry her to a **ridge post on a litter**. A **supply wagon** transfers her to the Keldor-edge Verdani lazaret before the following night. She has a measurable **fractured left wrist** and dangerous cold stress along mineral seams.
3. She was found **three days before waking**. How long she had been in the forest is unknown and no one can link the numbered marker to the impossible hall/Blackwood throne.
4. The wardens relay the description of a Grimnir traveler with a hammer and damaged wrist to **Elda's shelter** by routine incident report. **Sira** recognizes its possible referent, takes the next supply wagon and arrives the fourth morning after Brunti wakes; **Velen** arrives later by staffed stations while preserving records.
5. [Ch29's testimony](../manuscript/chapters/29-the-work-of-bringing-back.md) now correctly distinguishes the **known ordinary transport** from the **unknown path between the Silent Throne and the boundary marker**.

**Canon restraint:** No cause, geometry, portal rule, escape spell, prophecy, divine intervention or identity of the throne-figure was invented. The report's wardens, boundary marker, track and ridge-post transfer are **novel-only bridging fiction**, not new established Keldor locations. The Blackwood event order and follow-chain do not change.

**Canon restraint:** Her source game avatar remains the **female Grimnir warrior**. Terrace watch duties, individual escort decisions and personal shame are **novel biography** rather than additions to Morkahja character data. No gratuitous combat or human-body physiology has been introduced.
