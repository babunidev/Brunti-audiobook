## Character decision and causality

The story resumes at **Elda's staffed western shelter** after Chapter 23's successful rescue of the missing Verdani girl and separate recovery of the Meadowheart boy and injured Fringebound woman. The **girl travels onward safely with Sira and Velen**, while **Brunti refuses the southbound cart**. **Varun's outer-region map ends before the deep woods**, and he explicitly asks Brunti not to misattribute her invented roads to him. **Velen (they/them) will not join Blackwood**; they insist there is **no verified link** between Keldor's memory-like phenomena and the Grimnir memorial or Kaldric's Nekalos Mechanism. **Sira refuses to endorse Brunti's exploration** and continues caring for the living. The reunion with the girl's family is **still not claimed**.

Brunti **knows** that Darrik's safe transfer of the original composite memorial is already justified; the letter supporting his work was previously sent but not confirmed received. **Her renewed search is a mistake freely chosen**, not a prophecy, a requirement to save the child, or evidence of a functional universal memory-key. She passes tempting sealed containers, cannot read the Sylvan door and progressively loses the ability to connect her notes to spatial routes. The **voice-like impressions are unreliable perceptions** and never prove anything about home, the original two memorial stones, Kaldric, the Skyland or an external intelligence. The moral consequence is loss of the dependable return path, despite being free to take it at departure.

[Established Keldor lore](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Keldor.md) supports cursed mutable terrain and distorted perception; [Keldor Landmarks](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Places/Keldor_Landmarks.md) distinguishes the Sylvan Rune Gateway, twilight grove, blue-lit Enigma, roots, skull passage and bones; [Keldor Creatures](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Creatures/Keldor_Creatures.md) distinguishes the varied altered beings.
