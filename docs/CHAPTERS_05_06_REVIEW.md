**Specific source tension:** Encounter **20007** calls its gilded crest a sign of an *“Aevis royal house.”* The current [Aevis world culture](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Races/Aevis.md) establishes expert-led Resonance Councils and does **not** establish a monarchy or royal dynasty. The manuscript preserves the *physical crest* but frames the royal attribution as a merchant's unverified description. This is not a silent deletion of the source and does not establish a king.

**Second source tension:** Encounter **20040** names old “dworv tools.” The [world unresolved creature traditions](https://github.com/babunidev/MorkahjaWorld/blob/f56a7c3d2034652e1c5961f0ece9c3ef52b52b48/Creatures/Unresolved_Creature_Traditions.md) lists dworv as older terminology needing reconciliation. The manuscript retains **ancient mining cuts**, but does not canonize a new race or attribute the marks to a specific people.

**Dramatic hinge:** Sira receives Brunti's first full account of the failed memorial repair. The source fount and great tree are different local phenomena, not an explanation for the Skyland wall. The chapter ends in an actual refuge rather than revealing another secret key. The local keeper offers **ordinary** contact with craft-record keepers, preserving the intended transition to Chapter 7 without magical travel.
